1.2mi

Through the fleetTwenty cars. One page.

A real firm may run many more. The fleet comes from one Google Sheet, a row per car, and becomes a page for every car and every class. However many cars, the whole fleet counts as one page of the plan. A car that leaves out a rule (the deposit, the excess, the mileage) is refused before it reaches the site.

  • Every car, with filters that work even without JavaScript, search by make and model, sort by priceJunction 2

  • The fleet calendar: a fortnight of free and taken days, a row per carJunction 2
  • The spec dial: one control for seats, power, budget and dates, the cars lining up in one rowJunction 3

2.8mi

Pull in at the carEvery part of the price.

Each car's page carries its gallery at night, the firm's figures (shown only when given) and every rule a hire customer asks about. Pick dates on the calendar, days taken on the sheet greyed out, and the quote builds from the firm's own table: the weekend rate when it applies, delivery for the postcode or meet-and-greet at a named airport, extras, extra miles. The deposit stands apart, because it comes back.

  • "You can request this car if…": three answers read back against the car's rules, never a decisionJunction 2

4.1mi

SignalA request, in writing.

Hire firms check every licence before a key changes hands, so the site never says booked, confirmed or reserved. The request goes on WhatsApp with the dates in full, every part of the quote and a reference. The deposit link carries the same reference to Stripe, so the payment and the message match.

Hello Datum Hire, I'd like to request the Audi RS 6 Avant. From Friday 6 November 2026 at 18:00 to Monday 9 November 2026 at 18:00, delivery to SW11 4NQ. Weekend rate: £950 Delivery and collection, Zone one: £60 Total: £1,010 Deposit (returned): £2,500 Reference: audi-rs6-avant_2026-11-06_2026-11-09 I understand this is a request until you confirm.

Detour for the day itselfDriven, quietly.

On Signature the journey planner takes every stop with the customer's own time, works out the hours from the firm's rate and minimum, and sets a card for the wedding party to share. It never guesses a drive time; the firm confirms the route.

12.8mi

The way backRound the car, together.

At collection the driver and the customer walk round the car on the customer's phone: each panel, the fuel, the mileage, dated. At return they do it again, and the page lists what changed in plain words. It records what was seen and never decides a deposit. Then the customer follows the deposit home: held, car checked, returned, by reference only, never a name.
