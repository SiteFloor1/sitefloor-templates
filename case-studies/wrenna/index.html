How old is your dancer?

Tap an age. The curtain goes up.

The whole year, before the first class.

Which class, which day, what she wears, what a term costs, when half term is, and where to be on show day. A parent asks all of it before she's danced a step. Wrenna's website answers every one, on the phone in her hand.

The places left and the show-day details come from a sheet the school already keeps, so the site stays right without anyone touching it.

Places, from the sheet.

The school keeps one Google Sheet: a row for each class and time, the places left, and the day it was last changed. The site reads it every time a parent looks.

If nobody has touched it for a week, the site shows no number at all. A parent never sees "2 places left" that went a month ago.

Act three

Show day.

SignatureUp to twenty pages

Her call time, by code.

On show week each class's code goes into its WhatsApp group. A parent types it and gets the day in order: when to arrive, which door, the costume, the hair and when to collect.

The school fills one row a class in the same sheet. A row older than two weeks is never shown.
