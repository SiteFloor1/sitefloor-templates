How long a visit takes is the question a post never answers. Choose a room.

    A time, and someone to bring.

    Each time goes to the brand's own booking page. Pick one and the invitation is drawn on the phone, to send to a friend.

    It never says booked or few left: only the booking page knows.

    Not a ticket: everyone books their own time.

    What's left, and how long the queue is.

    Both come from the brand's own sheets, kept by the staff on a phone. An old sheet shows nothing at all rather than a guess.
