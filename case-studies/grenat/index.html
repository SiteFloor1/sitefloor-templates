Case study · Grand café and pâtisserie · Mayfair

Grenat

The salon, laid for her before she arrives.

A grand café's whole day on a phone: who is playing the harp, which pieces are left in the window, a stand made for every guest at the table, and a sitting given as a present. On a café's own site every word, price and photograph is its own, and nothing adds a running cost: it uses the café's Google Sheet, WhatsApp and Stripe links.

From eight in the morning until the candles.

The site keeps the café's hours. Through breakfast, luncheon and tea it is pith and gilt; at seven, when candlelight begins, it turns to garnet and velvet, before the page has even drawn, on every visit.

One pomegranate.

Grenat's colours come from a single fruit on velvet: the pale pith for the day, the dark rind for the night, the seeds for every accent, and the dried crown for the gilt. Repeated as a band, the crown is the house's motif, on the cards, the box and the stand.

The monogram sits under the crown. Titles are set in Caslon, the type of London's eighteenth-century printers; labels in small spaced capitals.
