# Case study source: laleh

- `laleh/index.html`: the social-first florist case study (her Instagram DMs: the page is one conversation, each
  chapter a customer's message set huge in italic on the left, often with the post they shared, and the reply from
  the right as Laleh's website answering it on a phone; the hero thread arrives once on load; live replies to "can
  you make this one?" (the real link check), "can u deliver today" (Laleh's cut-offs on London time) and "when's the
  next drop" (a countdown to Friday 7pm); the plans are pinned posts, one pin Starter, two Plus, three Signature;
  the speed is the message's "Delivered"; paper white with one peony, the messages the only round shapes; Tenor Sans
  and Spectral). Media (seven phone clips with posters, three phone stills, four demo photographs) live in the
  published artifact under `media/`. Clips: CDP screencast at 390×844 and 2x in full Chromium with `--lang=en-GB`
  (the headless shell ignores it, so date fields come out in US order), stitched with ffmpeg.
