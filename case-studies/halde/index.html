15min

A number on the door, never a guess.

The live count comes from the club's door system, through a Google Sheet. The site shows it with its age, and only while it's under fifteen minutes old. Older, or unreadable, and it goes back to the club's usual figure for the hour, and says so.

  • Live only from the club's own sheet, with the minutes since it was counted.
  • Usually the rest of the time: the club's own average for that hour of that weekday.
  • Places left on a class or a sauna slot follow the same rule. Without a fresh sheet, every session simply says Book in the app.
  • Never an estimate. A simulated count won't build on a client's site.

The price is the whole price.

Under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, the price a gym shows has to include every fee a member must pay to join. The kit works each headline out itself, from the monthly price, the minimum term and each fee. A membership listed without its fees won't build.
