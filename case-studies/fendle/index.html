    01Where we are, and when

    Asked under every post.

    "Where are you today?" The site answers before anyone types it: this week's stops from the stall's own sheet, the next one counting down, and the forecast for its hours.

    02The board

    Read it before the queue.

    Every item with its price and its allergens. When something sells out, the stall says so from a phone and the board strikes it through. One tap saves today's board as a picture, ready to post.

    03Parties

    Could you come to ours?

    Pick the day and the site checks the stall's party diary first: free, one booking already, or already promised. It never says booked. The details then go to WhatsApp, and the venue gets a set-up card it can copy.

    • Book us for your partyPlus
    • Parties and private hirePlus
    • What we need on the daySignature

      04The drop

      One Saturday only.

      Specials sell out the day they land. A drop gets its own date on the home page and a "Tell me" that adds people to the stall's WhatsApp list. The latest TikToks sit beside it, as links that open the app, never slow embeds.

      • The drop alertSignature
      • Latest postsPlus

      05Allergens

      Is there nut in it?

      The fourteen allergens, item by item, the stall's own words on what's shared at the counter, and its food hygiene rating linked to its listing on the Food Standards Agency's site. On the demo, the chart and the rating are labelled samples.

      • Allergens and food hygienePlus

      Hazelnut and strawberry: gluten, egg, milk, tree nuts, soya

      06Where to find us

      Four markets a week.

      Every regular market with its days and directions, this week first, and a cancelled market says so. On a stall's own site, the pitch finder adds its photo of the pitch and the line that tells you how to spot it.
