# Case study source: taroub

- `taroub/index.html`: the modest fashion case study (the exhibition: the site hung as a gallery show, "Where the hem
  falls", in seven rooms and an entrance. The entrance is the exhibit you can touch: the Layl full height, with the
  shop's five lengths drawn across her as hairlines to scale from the shoulder and an enlarged detail of the last
  inches beside her; the wall label asks her shoulder-to-floor and shoes and lights the length that clears her floor in
  saffron, with the gap, using the same rule as the kit's `hemAnswer`. Then a plan of the show; Room I the hang (the
  home page framed on a mat); II the hem line (a film on a phone on a plinth, and the hem photograph framed); III
  Before Eid, worked out live in the visitor's browser from the same lunar windows and the demo's cut-offs; IV the
  family's list (film); V complete the look (laptop screen and film); VI a private appointment and the length guide
  (two phones); VII the collection; the plans as three admission tickets; catalogue notes (the numbers); the exit
  through the shop. Every exhibit carries a museum wall label. Warm ivory walls, ink frames, one saffron accent;
  Shippori Mincho and Afacad (Taroub's own), self-hosted with size-matched stand-ins). Media (three phone films with
  posters, three phone stills, three laptop screens, five of the demo's photographs, the fonts) live in `media/` and the
  published artifact. Phone films: CDP screencast at 390×844 and 2x in Chromium with `--lang=en-GB`, H.264; stills and
  films without the demo bar; nothing plays until asked.
