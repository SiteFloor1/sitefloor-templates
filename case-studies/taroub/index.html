III

Before Eid.

The busiest weeks of the year turn on one question: will it arrive in time? The answer below was worked out today, in your browser, the way Taroub's site does it.

Today, at Taroub

The last UK order date for Eid

Eid follows the moon sighting. Taroub counts back from the first day it may fall.

Eid last order dates Taroub, 2026

The shop's own cut-offs, counted back from the first day Eid is expected, so the promise holds if it's announced late. When no Eid is ahead in the calendar, the section steps aside rather than guess.

IV

The family's list.

Eid is bought for the whole family. One link goes round; each adds a name, a piece and a length; Taroub gets one order.

The family's list Taroub, 2026

Kept only in the link itself: nothing is saved anywhere. "Send our order" puts every name, piece, length and the total into one WhatsApp message.

V

Complete the look.

The abaya, the hijab worn with it and the inner, in her length, with one total. The look follows her along the foot of every page.

The look and its bar Taroub, 2026

Sent to the shop on WhatsApp, or paid through Taroub's own Stripe link. A payment link has a fixed price, so it only shows while the look matches it; add the slip and she sends it instead.
