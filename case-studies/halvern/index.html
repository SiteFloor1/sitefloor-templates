Ground floor · the home page

The street door.

Halvern's home page opens on the building itself, in daylight, with a brass lift plate over its doors. Each lamp is a floor, lit where a home on it is free.

Today's homesStarter

Read from the agent's own Google Sheet each time the page opens: "Five homes free now. Updated 09:15 today."

On every card

Rent, size in m² and sq ft, the holding deposit and deposit worked out within the legal caps, council tax band, energy rating, parking, broadband, pets.

Book a viewing

One tap writes the WhatsApp message: the home's name, building, reference and rent.

When it's out of date

If the sheet goes a day and a half without an update, no home shows as free and the page says to call. Nobody books a flat that let last week.

Floor 1 · Fees, in the menu of every page

Every fee, with VAT.

Letting agents in England must show their fees and name the schemes that protect their clients. Halvern's site keeps them one tap away, on every page, on every plan.

Floor 2 · Residences

Every building, floor by floor.

A schedule like the one framed in a porter's lodge: three buildings side by side, the free homes on each floor with their rent, size and bedrooms, the let ones quiet.

From the same sheet as the home page. A building without that floor shows a hatched cell; a porter's floor says so.

Floor 3 · Areas and the journal

The streets around it.

A page for each neighbourhood the agent lets in, with what's free there now; and articles that answer what tenants and owners ask, every rule dated against GOV.UK.

AreasPlus

The client's own photograph of each neighbourhood, or a licensed, credited one until then.

What you pay to move in. Your deposit. Letting your home after 1 May 2026. Only GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk are accepted as sources.

4
Floor 4 · For owners

What would my home let for?

The owner describes the home in one sentence. It reaches the agent on WhatsApp as a valuation request. The site never guesses a rent: a director replies with one, in writing.

The valuationSignature

Bedrooms, kind of home, size, bathrooms, address, outside space, furnished, the service. Nothing kept by the page.

What we managePlus

The homes counted from the agent's own buildings, never typed in; a managed year month by month.

Onboarding, storiesSignature

The steps and the checklist in the agent's own words; stories with the owner's own figures.
