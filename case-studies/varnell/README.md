# Case study source: varnell

- `varnell/index.html`: the car detailing case study (a car brochure: the cover is Varnell's 3D SUV on a studio
  cyclorama in front of the name set wide, repainted from the demo's paint chart, "approximate on screen"; brochure
  chapters under mono running heads with page numbers, Exterior p.02 to Technical data p.09; the 3D correction and
  film clips as dark plates; a live film price list (package by car) and a live collection check from the demo's own
  data; the plans are trim levels with their standard equipment, each on the same car in another paint; the speed is
  the back page's technical data, "1.4 s to first paint"; Michroma, Geist and Geist Mono on a pale studio grey).
  Media (the SUV rendered from the shared 3D model in the eight chart colours, five clips with posters, the demo's
  headlight photograph) live in the published artifact under `media/`. Phone clips: CDP screencast at 390×844 and 2x
  in full Chromium with `LANG=en_GB.UTF-8` (needed for UK date fields); 3D clips: element screenshots of the stage
  with Playwright's clock stepped 1/30 s per frame. Car renders: three.js with RoomEnvironment, a shadow-catcher
  floor and `VSMShadowMap`, transparent WebP.
