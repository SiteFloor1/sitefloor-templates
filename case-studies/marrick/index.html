The week, open to book.

The first thing on Marrick's site is the trainer's own week, from a Google Sheet he fills in between clients. Spoken-for hours stay quiet; the five open ones are printed in ink.

Tap one and it becomes a WhatsApp message for that hour. If the sheet is out of date, no hours show at all, only “Message for times”.

    The first screen, on a phone.

    “The week is mostly spoken for.” Five hours are still open.

    Is the first session different?
    Yes. We talk first, you fill in a short health questionnaire, and I check how you move before any training.
    I haven't trained for years. Is that all right?
    Most people I train start that way. The first sessions are lighter than you'd expect.
    What if I need to cancel?
    Cancel 24 hours ahead and there's no charge. Later than that, the session counts.
    How soon will I see a difference?
    It depends on you, your week and how often we train. I won't promise a number; we'll agree what to look for and check it every four weeks.
