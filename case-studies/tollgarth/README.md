- `tollgarth/index.html`: the garden designer case study (from first light: the page is one day in Richmond, read by
  the sun. The hero is the demo's own example garden drawn in 3D by the kit's sun code, with the shadows of the house
  and fences at the hour you set; it opens at the real time in Richmond (dark at night), with Today, 21 June,
  21 December and "Watch the day", sunrise to sunset in twelve seconds, and the ground of the hero follows the light.
  The contents are a true horizontal sundial for Richmond's latitude, each hour line a chapter, with today's shadow
  on it live; the chapters are hours (VI the year, VIII the studio's month, X the sample table, XII where the sun
  falls, II care on the year, IV ready by, VI do I need permission?), then "after dark" for the other pages; the plans
  are three days, 21 December (Starter), 20 March (Plus), 21 June (Signature), each a dial of that day's hours of light
  worked out live; the speed is "before the shadow moves", how far a fence's shadow moves while the home page loads.
  Copper beech is the one colour; Cinzel and the demo's Alegreya on stone). Media (three phone clips with posters,
  twelve phone stills, the demo's meadow, dusk and path-light photographs) live in `media/` and the published
  artifact. Phone clips: CDP screencast at 390×844 and 2x, resampled to 30 fps, H.264; stills: Playwright at 390×844
  and 2x, the demo ribbon hidden. The kit's sun code (`sun-draw.js`, `sun-study.js`, core `sun.js`) is bundled inline
  with esbuild.
