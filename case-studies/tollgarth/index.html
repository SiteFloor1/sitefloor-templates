From firstlight.

The website of a garden studio, read the way a garden is: by the sun.

One day, seven hours.

Each line on this dial is a page of Tollgarth's site, set at the hour it does its work. It is drawn for Richmond, where the studio is.

VI6 am · First light

The year, open at this month.

Twelve plants from the studio's own gardens stand side by side. The visitor's month opens by itself, its name across the photograph; any other opens with a tap. The site is never out of season, and nobody has to update it.

VIII8 am · At the studio

The studio's month.

What Tollgarth is designing, building, planting and caring for now, following the month chosen above. The next design and build start sits beside it, and takes itself down once it is out of date.

Starter · the home page

X10 am · The yard

The sample table.

Granite, reclaimed brick, oak, gravel, moss: the materials the studio builds with, laid out as one board. Touch a sample and it becomes the garden it was laid in. Tick a few and ask for samples on WhatsApp.

Plus · its own page

XIINoon · The showpiece

Where the sun falls.

The question every garden starts with. On the home page, the studio's example garden turns to face another way or shows itself in December. On its own page the visitor draws their own: its size, which way it faces, the house and the fences. The plan shows the hours of sun across it on the 21st of any month, and the shadows at any hour, from above or in 3D.

The garden at the top of this page is drawn by the same code.

Signature · home and its own page

II2 pm · The round

Care, laid on the year.

Each maintenance plan set out over twelve months: which months the studio comes and what it does in each, with its own price when it publishes one. "Ask about this plan" sends the garden's size and postcode on WhatsApp.

Signature · its own page

IV4 pm · Planning ahead

Ready by.

Choose the month the garden should be finished. The calendar works back through the studio's own weeks for design, the wait for a build slot and the build itself, and gives the day to get in touch.

VI6 pm · Over the fence

Do I need permission?

Fences, decking, garden rooms and front gardens, in plain words from GOV.UK, with the day it was checked and both sources. A guide, not a decision.

Tollgarth is a fictional Richmond garden studio made to show the Sitefloor garden kit. Its gardens, months, plans, prices and articles are demo data.
