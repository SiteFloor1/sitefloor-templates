# Case study source: marlie

- `marlie/index.html`: the teeth whitening case study (the social six: the page opens on a cosmetic dentist's planning
  screen, a real smile in black and white with the guides drawing themselves on it, the midline, the commissure line
  at its measured 2.6°, the smile arc along the edges, and the six front teeth 13 12 11 | 21 22 23 marked with their
  widths against the front tooth; touch a tooth and the readout names it and its chapter. The midline then runs down
  the whole page and each chapter is one of the six, numbered as a dentist numbers it (FDI), opening on the six with
  its own tooth marked and sitting on its own side of the line: 11 the booth strip, 21 tell me what you'd change
  (live, with the demo's words), 12 nothing it can't keep (GDC lines and a live claims check that refuses shade,
  permanence, guarantee, laser and specialist promises as the kit does), 22 when's the big day (live, the demo's
  timings), 13 visit by visit and aftercare, 23 smiles with consent and arriving; the premolars list the rest of the
  site; the plans are the golden proportion, Signature 1.618, Plus 1, Starter 0.618, drawn at those widths; the speed
  is read off a rule in tenths of a second; the demo's own fonts, Reddit Sans and Brygada 1918 italic, with Reddit Mono
  for every measurement). Media (the smile photograph, three phone clips with posters, six phone stills) live in
  `media/` and the published artifact. Phone captures: full Chromium at 390×844 and 2x with `--lang=en-GB`, the demo
  ribbon hidden; the strip clip recorded with CSS animations at a quarter speed (CDP `Animation.setPlaybackRate`)
  and played back at full speed, so it's smooth.

  Photograph credits: `media/smile.webp` (the hero, every chapter's crop and the closing figure) is "Close Up Photo of
  Person's Lips" by Amir SeilSepour, a model photograph, free under the Pexels licence:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/close-up-photo-of-person-s-lips-11956948/. Every other image and clip is a capture of
  the smile demo, whose photographs are credited in `sites/demos/smile/img/CREDITS.md`.
