Tell me.

Before you drive, the examiner asks a tell me question. Learners ask theirs before they book. These three answers come on every plan, Starter included.

Tell me 1Starter

“How much is a lesson?”

Manual and automatic, side by side. A block of ten saves £20: the site works the saving out from the instructor's own prices.

Tell me 2Starter

“Do you come to me?”

A postcode, and a straight answer from the instructor's own list of districts. Try one.

Wenlow covers eight districts, east of the City and over the river.

Tell me 3Starter

“What happens on the day?”

The practical test in gov.uk's order: the eyesight check, show me and tell me, general driving, a reversing exercise, independent driving. Each step in a line, with gov.uk linked for the detail.

Show me.

The show me question comes while you're driving. Plus shows the week's free lessons, the DVSA's own readiness check and up to five pages.

Show me 1Plus

“A free lesson this week.”

The instructor keeps a Google Sheet of free times; the site shows the next three, drops any that have passed and hides the list if the sheet goes stale. One tap writes the message.

    Tap a lesson to see the message it writes.On Wenlow's site it opens in WhatsApp, ready to send.

    Show me 2Plus

    “Am I ready to book?”

    Ready to Pass?, the DVSA's own five points, answered yes or not yet. Not yet tells you what the next lessons are for. The wording is gov.uk's, with the link.

    Plus has up to five of these pages; Signature has them all. An instructor's badge number and pass rate appear only when the instructor gives them, a pass rate with its dates; the site refuses "pass first time", "guaranteed" and "fastest" in anything the school writes.

    Independent driving.

    About twenty minutes on your own, following a sat nav or the signs. Signature adds the parts a learner comes back to between lessons.

    Independent 1Signature

    “How am I doing?”

    Every skill at the DVSA's five levels, the last lesson and what to practise, from the instructor's sheet after each lesson. A learner opens it with a code; no name is ever on the sheet, and a sheet with a name on it shows nothing.

    Try it in the demo with the code WNL4821.

    Independent 2Signature

    “When could I pass, and what will it cost?”

    The card at the top of this page, on the home page. Weeks to test-ready at the hours a week you choose, and the cost from the school's own block or intensive price plus gov.uk's fees, rounded and labelled an estimate. If the fees go six months without a check, they drop out with a note.

    Back to the card

    Independent 3Signature

    “Mirror, signal, then what?”

    A journal of short notes from the instructor: the fault they see most, the hour before a test. The kind of page people find from a search and keep.

    And the day the plates come off.

    A Passed page, with your pupils' own photographs from test day. It needs real ones, so it's on client sites only and the demo leaves it off.
