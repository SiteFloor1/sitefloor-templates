Case study · Lingerie boutique, Mayfair

Seen at midnight. Fitted by Friday.

Marese sells French lace from a first-floor salon in Mayfair. Her customers find it on a phone, late, in private. The site is made for that moment, and for what she does next.

Starter

She sees the piece before the price.

The campaign first, large: a woman wearing the collection, nothing laid over the lace. Then the new season, every piece by its full name, Calloway · Bralette · Smoke, with its colours and its price.

  • Shop by category: bras, briefs, suspenders, bodies, robes, bridal
  • A private fitting, asked for on WhatsApp
  • Gift wrap with a handwritten card; discreet packaging in her words
Plus

He doesn't know her size. He doesn't have to.

Tell us about her: what she wears, the budget, the occasion, in one WhatsApp message. The shop sets pieces aside. And on any piece, she can drop a hint to whoever she chooses.

Plus

February arrives a fortnight early.

From late January the home page belongs to Valentine's: the campaign, the edit to give and the edit to hint at, and a countdown to the shop's own last order time. After it, she can still collect. Then it switches itself off. Christmas does the same.

Marese's last Valentine's order: Thursday 11 February, 4pm.

Plus

Three pieces, waiting upstairs.

She taps "Have it at my fitting" on up to three pieces, anywhere on the site. When she asks for a fitting, they're already in the message, so the fitter has them in her size before she arrives.

  • The fitting, Under the dress, Bridal and Buying for her pages
  • A fit finder: "the band rides up" and what it usually means
Signature

Her sizes, in eight characters.

After a fitting she's given a private code. Whoever she shares it with sees the sizes her fitter wrote down, balconette 32D, plunge 32DD, and can ask the shop for pieces in them. The sheet behind it holds no names.

  • The gift concierge: her birthday and your anniversary in his calendar, with reminders
  • Wedding dates: one date in, every fitting worked back from it
Signature

After seven, the salon is hers.

The private evening: the shop closes for one client. Its free evenings come from a sheet the shop keeps; she chooses one and asks, and the deposit is the shop's own Stripe link. A bride asks for the same evening for her bridesmaids, names and wedding date included.

If the sheet goes quiet, no evening is offered. She's asked to message instead.

Signature

Close enough to touch.

A loupe follows her finger across the lace: the twist of the threads, the scallop cut by hand. Beside it, the shop's own words on how it's made. Then a page for every collection and every piece, and guides for fuller busts, stockings and care.
