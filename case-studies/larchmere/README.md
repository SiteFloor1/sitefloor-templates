# Case study source: larchmere

- `larchmere/index.html`: the wedding film case study (read by the sun's height: the page opens on Larchmere's film
  edge to edge with a live headline worked out from the sun's real elevation over Bibury now ("In Bibury the sun is
  8° up. Golden hour begins today at 15:34", "It's golden hour in Bibury now…"), and a degree scale down the left edge
  as the index with a glowing sun at its current height; each chapter sits at a height, +40° the film, +20° your date,
  +6° golden hour (with a live golden-hour field for any date), 0° the day, −4° your film, and its ground darkens from
  ivory to night; the plans at −6° are three lights, Daylight (Starter), Golden hour (Plus), Blue hour (Signature),
  each topped with a cut of that sky; the speed is "a degree every four minutes"; the demo's own fonts, Sorts Mill
  Goudy and Urbanist). The sun is NOAA's calculation, as in the kit's `lib/sun.js`. Media (the demo's wide and tall
  film with posters, three phone clips with posters, two phone stills) live in the published artifact under `media/`.
  Phone clips: Playwright screenshots at 390×844 and 2x in full Chromium with `--lang=en-GB` (UK date fields), one per
  step and one per typed letter, stitched with ffmpeg (the CDP screencast came out at 1x).
