I · Table

Six screens, then the door.

Every planThe home page

  1. One piece, very largeThe Solitaire, its name, its price as the house sets it, and "See it in person". No headline.
  2. The collections as doorsEngagement, wedding bands, anniversary, pearls: each a tall photograph of the piece worn, each opening its own page.
  3. The boxOne band of the house's colour, the box itself and the monogram.
  4. From the benchOne commission, from the drawing to the finished ring, with the weeks between. On Signature.
  5. Visit and the appointmentThe address and hours beside the request, in the one bold button.
  6. The footerThe address, the hours and every page, quietly.

On a phone the whole home page fits in under six screens. Reviews, questions, the room and the birthstones live on Plan your visit and their own pages, so the first impression stays quiet.

One colour, used rarely.

Conch pink, from the inside of a shell. It appears on the box, the appointment button and the phone's bottom bar, and almost nowhere else, so when it appears it means something.

V · Culet · where it all meets

Nothing guessed. Nothing slow.

Every planThe house's facts, a phone's speed

Measured on the demo's home page on a fresh server: 98 on the first, cold load and 98 again. Every page passes an accessibility scan, and the site still works with JavaScript turned off: a piece links to its own page, a request is a plain WhatsApp link.

Only the house's facts.

Metal, stones, carat, cut and price come from the house's own entry. A field left empty shows nothing; nothing is filled in for it.

Claims where they belong.

Words such as hallmarked, GIA, conflict-free or lab-grown are refused anywhere but the field made for them, and a demo can never carry them. Aldous Marr's specs say they are a fictional house's.

Requests, never bookings.

Every message asks: an appointment, a hint, a commission, a party, a gift. The house replies to confirm. Nothing is ordered or booked by the website alone.

Set in three sizes

Choose how deep it goes.

Every plan is on a 12-month minimum. Any single feature can be added to a smaller plan, and extra pages one at a time.
