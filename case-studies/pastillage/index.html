I

Is our date free?

The first tier, on every plan.

  • Your date, checked

    A couple picks a date and sees free, one place left or taken, from a Google Sheet the studio keeps.

  • The commissions

    Each cake numbered and named, with its tiers, how many it serves and a from-price.

  • How far ahead

    How far ahead to book each kind of cake, in the studio's own words.

  • Allergens, before the order

    The allergens in every flavour from the studio's own chart, and what its kitchen handles.

II

How many are you feeding?

The second tier, from Plus.

  • The cake, sized

    A couple types their guest count; the site answers in tiers, portions and a from-price, from the studio's own chart.

  • Choose by the slice

    Six flavours, each a layer of one cut slice with its season, so a couple chooses by looking.

  • The tasting box

    Sold through the studio's own Stripe link and posted out; no shop software to pay for.

  • The brief, on WhatsApp

    Date, guests, tiers, a flavour for each and the venue arrive as one message, ready to quote from.

III

Will you bring it to us?

The third tier, on Signature.

  • Delivery, by postcode

    A venue postcode gives the area, the delivery charge and when the studio arrives to set up.

  • Design it with us

    The way to meet, the days that suit and the event date arrive as one WhatsApp message; the studio offers a time.

  • In season for your month

    Pick the month of the event and see which flavours are on then.

  • A page for each kind of cake

    Weddings, celebrations, corporate and dessert tables, each with its own prices and commissions.
