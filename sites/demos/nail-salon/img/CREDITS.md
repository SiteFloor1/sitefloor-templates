# Photo credits (Verrall demo)

Every photo is from Unsplash under the Unsplash Licence (https://unsplash.com/license), cropped and given one warm
daylight grade. Verrall is fictional; a client's site uses the salon's own photographs.

**Changed for the demo, said plainly:** the four `hand-light-*.webp` shades are the same photograph of the same hand
with the nails recoloured from a hand-drawn mask (Starter and Plus fade between real photographs of one hand in
each shade; on a client site those are the salon's own shots). The `*-mask.png` files are those masks. The bottles on
the colour wall and the tray are softened so no brand's label can be read. Two grounds are recoloured to
the demo's umber, light for light, with the hands left as photographed: the maroon behind `hand-deep.webp` and the
slate behind the pearl chrome set. `finish-cat-eye.webp` is white-balanced to take out a magenta cast.
