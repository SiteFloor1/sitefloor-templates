# Photo and film credits: Velvet Spoon demo

Free stock, checked 28 October 2026: Pexels photos and video under the Pexels License (https://www.pexels.com/license/),
Unsplash photos under the Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license; none are Unsplash+). They are other businesses'
chocolates, gelato and cakes, used to show the kit; the footer and demo ribbon label the whole site fictional.
Cropped and sized to where each one is shown (3:4 for the counter, 4:3 for page links, 2:1 for the band, 16:9 and 4:5 for the film), given one shared grade (a little
warmer, slightly less saturated, deeper shadows) so they read as one shoot, and compressed to WebP. The box pieces
(`p-*.webp`) are single chocolates cut out of two photos by the same photographer, plus one truffle.
