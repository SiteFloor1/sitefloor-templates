# Wedding films: customer questions

What this trade's couples ask before they get in touch (kit `weddingfilm`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Luxury wedding videographer and photographer (weddingfilm kit, branch `trade/wedding-film`)
Researched 2 Nov 2026 from real UK studios' FAQ pages (Sinclair Wedding Films, Hampshire; Trinity Films) and directory
listings (Bridebook, Rock My Wedding, Guides for Brides). Every price, timing and policy on a client site is the studio's own.

| Couples ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Are you free on our date? | `date-check`: the studio's diary sheet; free, held or booked; the date and venue on WhatsApp; never "free" from an unreadable sheet |
| Can we see a whole film, from a venue like ours? | `studio-film` (the work first), `films-page` (Plus) with venue chips and a link to the whole film |
| What's in each package, and what does it cost? | `wf-packages` (hours, films, photographs, price, deposit), `wf-builder` (Plus) with extras and the studio's total |
| How long until we get it? | `delivery-times`: each part a number of weeks after the wedding, with the days once a date is in |
| What's the deposit, and when is the rest due? | `packages.deposit`; the builder's deposit link (the studio's own Stripe) |
| Do you travel? Abroad? | the questions; `places` can include places abroad for the light |
| Drone? | the builder's drone extra ("where the venue and the airspace allow it") and the questions |
| Do we choose the music? Are the vows and speeches recorded? | the questions |
| Will you be in the way? Do you need a meal? | the questions |
| When should we do portraits? When's sunset? | `sunset-line` (Plus, on the date check), `light-planner` (Signature): calculated for the date and place |
| Where is our film, and when is the rest due? | `film-progress` (Signature): footage in, edit, colour, sound, delivered, and the trailer's date, by the code the studio sends |
| What happens when on the day? When do we do portraits? | `day-planner` (Signature): the day built from the ceremony time, portraits in that evening's golden hour |
| Can you fly a drone at our venue? | `venues-page` (Signature): each venue's rule, the UK rules with sources |
| Can we have our song? Will the church let you film? | `music-page` (Signature): licensing and church rules, sourced |
| Who will actually be there? | `team` (Signature) |
| Do you use AI to edit? Will you share our film? | the questions, in the studio's words |
