# Clothing brands (menswear, running and outerwear): customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `wear`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Menswear: running and outerwear brand (new `wear` kit, trade/menswear)
Researched 29 Oct 2026 from UK run and outerwear brands (Represent and its 247 line, SOAR Running, product pages at
UK stockists) and the questions their FAQs and product pages answer. The kit should also fit a cosy-essentials brand.
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What size am I? What are the measurements per size? | `fit-room`: flat measurements per size (chest, back length, sleeve), from the client's spec sheet |
| How does it sit on the body? Oversized, regular, room for a layer? | `fit-room` fit note per size; model's height and size worn, only if the client supplies them |
| When is the next drop, and what time? | `drop-clock` (Google Sheet: drop name, date, time, pieces) |
| It's sold out. Will it come back? Can I get told? | `drop-clock` stock states (in stock / few left / sold out / restock list), Google Form waitlist |
| Is it waterproof or just water-resistant? Windproof? Breathable? | `fabric-notes`: only figures the client can back with the mill's spec (e.g. a stated hydrostatic head); otherwise plain words ("water-repellent finish") |
| How do I wash it and keep it repelling rain? | `fabric-notes` care lines; questions |
| How long is delivery, and do you ship abroad? | `shop-links` delivery block; questions |
| Can I return it, and how long do I have? | questions (the client's own policy; online orders carry a 14-day right to cancel from delivery under the Consumer Contracts Regulations, checked on which.co.uk 29 Oct 2026) |
| Where can I try it on? Is there a pop-up or showroom, and when is it open? | `visit` / Plan your visit, `popups` (dated pop-up list from a Sheet) |
| When and where is the run club? Is it free? What pace? Do I need to sign up? | `run-club` calendar (Sheet), WhatsApp "I'm in", add-to-calendar |
| What should I wear for today's weather? | `wear-today` (Open-Meteo, the brand's own paid plan; fails closed) |
| What's new on TikTok and Instagram? | `feed` (Behold for Instagram; TikTok as linked posts, no paid API) |
