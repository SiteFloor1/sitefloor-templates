# Domestic and commercial cleaning: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `cleaning`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Cleaning companies, homes and offices (new `cleaning` kit, demo Linnet) — proposed 15 Nov 2026, awaiting the owner
- **Design process:** three one-screen directions (A Visit, B Keyholder, C Before nine); no build until one is approved.
- **Starter:** services and coverage, a postcode check (the client's districts), keys and access, questions, visit.
- **Plus:** the instant quote by size (bedrooms, bathrooms, how often; the client's own hours and rates), what every
  clean includes room by room, the end-of-tenancy checklist, the next free day under the postcode check (client's Sheet).
- **Signature:** the visit builder (extras, frequency, postcode and live free days, WhatsApp summary, calendar file),
  the move-out planner (check-out date, book-by date, before-we-arrive list, the client's own re-clean terms), out of
  hours for offices (your hours against ours, zones and frequencies, site-survey request; no prices shown), the schedule
  as calendar files.
- **Alternatives:** a visit log by code; short-let turnovers; a live key log by number; carpet and upholstery by the room;
  the home in 3D (parked: heavy for what it sells).
- **Client-only:** before/after of a clean (a real pair of one room from the client).
- **Honesty:** insurance, vetting, memberships and guarantees only as the client states them; a deposit is never
  promised; deposit rules linked to gov.uk with the date checked; quotes are a guide until the client confirms.
- **Live data fails closed:** free days older than 48 hours or missing show "ask us for a day", never a date.
- **Running cost:** £0 (the client's Sheet, WhatsApp, calendar files made in the browser).
