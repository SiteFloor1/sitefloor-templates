# Domestic and commercial cleaning: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `cleaning`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

Gathered 15 Nov 2026 from: the FAQ and booking pages of UK cleaning agencies and platforms (national booking apps,
London and regional independents, a franchise network); end-of-tenancy price guides and checklists published by
cleaning firms, letting agents, a professional body for agents and a deposit protection scheme; office-cleaning
quote pages and contract guides from commercial contractors and a law firm's guide to cleaning contracts; and
customer reviews of UK cleaning companies on a public review site (good and bad). What customers complain about
most in reviews: no-shows and late cancellations without notice, a different cleaner each visit, being chased for
payments already made, and slow or no response when a clean wasn't done.

## Honesty rules for this trade
- Insurance, vetting (DBS, references, right to work), memberships and guarantees appear only as the client states
  them; never an invented accreditation, logo or "fully insured" line. A demo labels its own as "(demo)".
- A deposit is never promised. End-of-tenancy wording says what the clean covers and what the client's re-clean
  terms are; deposit rules link to gov.uk, with the date checked.
- Prices and times come only from the client's own rate sheet; a quote is a guide until the client confirms it.
