# Cafés: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `restaurant`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Café (restaurant kit, look `counter`, demo Morrow) — built 28 Oct 2026
- **Questions:** `docs/trades/cafe/questions.md`.
- **Starter:** the menu with breakfast, brunch and lunch sittings and every allergen; "On the counter" (each bake tagged
  fresh out, baked or sold out from the sheet, the coffee board beside it); delivery; the PDF menu; open now; the loyalty card; table
  requests (which can be limited to weekend brunch).
- **Plus:** takeaway orders; Can I work here? (wifi, sockets, laptops, dogs, kids, buggies, access, toilets; today's
  laptop-friendly hours from the café's own numbers); the regulars' board (guest roaster, what's playing, dog of the week);
  the menu page; events.
- **Signature:** Your usual (the showpiece: a glass that fills as you choose, the café's own prices, remembered on the
  phone, ordered ahead on WhatsApp); the glass case (bakes light up when a tray comes out; put one aside); how busy is
  it now; the seat finder; the brunch builder (pre-orders with the table request).
- **Also:** the shared loyalty card works for cafés (a menu item and "N times a week"). **Not yet:** a beans
  subscription (after the shared pay-link merges).
- **Setting up a client:** a Google Sheet with `kind,name,price,note,updated_at` (special, soldout, fresh, busy, board);
  the café's price list for Your usual (with each milk's allergens from its label); its busyness from its till; a floor
  sketch for the seat finder; its counter photo for the hero (we mark where each bake sits).
