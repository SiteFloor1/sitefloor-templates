# Facialists and skin clinics: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `skin`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Independent facialist and skin clinic (skin kit)
Gathered 15 Nov 2026 from the kinds of sources clients actually read: UK independent facialists' and skin clinics' FAQ
pages (London, Somerset, Essex and regional clinics), their booking pages on small-business booking apps (what they ask
before a first visit), pre-care and aftercare sheets for peels and microneedling, Treatwell-style listings (patch-test
and consultation slots, times and "from" prices), Google reviews (what clients praise or regret), and magazine
round-ups of London facials (what readers are told to ask). Every answer on a site is the clinic's own wording from its
`site.json`; the site suggests a treatment by concern, never diagnoses, and never promises a result.

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Which facial is right for my skin? I don't know what to book. | skin consultation (Signature): pick what you'd like to change, see the clinic's own suggestion, time, "from" price, patch test and downtime, then send it on WhatsApp; "your facialist confirms at your consultation" on every answer. Starter and Plus: the menu grouped by concern in the clinic's words |
| How long does it take? What actually happens in the hour? | treatment menu (Starter): every treatment's time; the treatment hour (Signature): each stage in the clinic's words |
| How much is it? Is the consultation charged? Is there a deposit? | menu and prices (Starter): "from" prices the clinic sets, consultation fee or free, deposit by the clinic's own Payment Link |
| Do I need a patch test? How long before? Is it free? | patch-test line on every treatment that needs one, in the clinic's words (48 hours is the common rule on the booking pages read); patch-test date from your appointment date, with a calendar file (Starter extra) |
| Is there downtime? Can I go to work or an event straight after? Can I wear make-up? | each treatment's downtime line in the clinic's words (from "none" for a hydrating facial to redness or light flaking after a peel) |
| How often should I come? Is it a course? | course planner (Signature): the clinic's spacing (e.g. every 4–6 weeks for a peel), your dates worked out and added to your calendar; the menu says "single" or "course of n" |
| What should I stop before? (retinol, acids, waxing, sun, other treatments) | before-your-visit page (Plus): the clinic's own list and how many days before; the course planner marks the stop-from date |
| Aftercare: what do I do afterwards, and when can I use my products again? | aftercare (Plus): the clinic's own instructions; reminders on the right days as calendar files (Plus extra) |
| I have a wedding or event: when should I start? When is the last safe facial? | course planner (Signature): enter the date, see the start date and the last appointment the clinic allows before it |
| Is it safe in pregnancy, on acne medication, with sensitive skin or rosacea? | consultation and policies page: only the clinic's own words, and "please tell us at booking"; the site gives no medical advice |
| Who will treat me? What are their qualifications and insurance? Are they on a register? | about the facialist (every plan): qualifications, insurance, any accredited register, all from the clinic and linked where public |
| Is the clinic licensed? | footer and policies: the borough's special treatment licence where the clinic is in a London borough that licenses facials (London Local Authorities Act 1991), from the clinic |
| Do you do injectables, fillers, skin boosters? | menu: only if the clinic offers them through a named prescriber (out of scope for the demo); otherwise "we don't, and here's why" in the clinic's words |
| Can I see results? | results (client-only, Signature): the clinic's own consented photographs, same light, unedited, each with the treatment named; never stock |
| Do you sell the products you use? Can I order refills? | shop page (Plus): the clinic's own range, ordered by WhatsApp or Payment Link |
| Can I buy a facial as a gift? Is there a membership or course price? | gift vouchers and memberships (shared features, Plus) |
| Is there parking? Step-free access? A changing space? Can I come at lunch? | Plan your visit (every plan) and the live open status |
| What's your cancellation policy? Can I move my appointment? | policies (every plan), in the clinic's words; link to its booking app |
| Do you treat men? Under-18s? | policies: the clinic's own rule; under-18s follow the law for the treatment and the clinic's insurance |

peel and microneedling pre-care sheets; booking-app listings with patch-test and consultation slots; Google reviews;
London facial round-ups in beauty magazines. Regulator sources and dates: see `playbook.md` (UK rules).
