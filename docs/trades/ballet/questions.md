# Children's dance and ballet schools: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `ballet`, not built yet). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Children's ballet and dance school (design round 1, branch `trade/ballet`)
Written 17 Nov 2026 from the questions parents ask on real UK schools' class pages, FAQ pages and listings
(Happity, Hoop and IDTA school-finder pages). The customer is the parent; the dancer is two to eighteen.
Every class, fee, date, policy, qualification and exam board on a client site comes from the client. Safeguarding,
DBS checks and first aid are shown only as the school's own statements; exam boards (RAD, ISTD, IDTA, BBO) only if
the school names them.

| Parents ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| My child is four. Which class, which day, what time? | BAL-1 class finder by age (Starter): age in, the class, day, time and length out |
| Is there a free or paid trial? How do I book it? | BAL-3 trial class request (Plus): age, preferred day, WhatsApp message to the school |
| How much is it? Per term or per class? Sibling discount? Do I pay if we miss a week? | Fees and terms page, in the school's words; never a figure we invent |
| When does term start and finish? Half term? Is there class on bank holidays? | BAL-2 term dates (Starter): one calendar file for the whole term |
| What does she wear? Colours by class? Where do I buy it? Hair? | BAL-4 uniform list per class (Plus), with the school's supplier link |
| Can I watch? Where do I wait? Is there a parents' viewing week? | the questions and Plan your visit |
| Who teaches? Are they qualified, DBS-checked, first-aid trained? | Teachers page; the school's own safeguarding statement |
| Do you do exams? Which board? When is she ready? | Exams page (only if the school runs exams, in its words) |
| When is the show? How do I get tickets? Can I film it? | BAL-6 show tickets via the school's own ticket link (Signature); filming policy in the school's words |
| Are boys welcome? | the class finder (classes open to all) and the school's wording |
| My child has additional needs. Can she join? | the questions, in the school's words, and a WhatsApp question |
| Do you do holiday camps or birthday parties? | Holiday camps and Birthday parties pages |
| Where is it, parking, drop-off, toilets, changing rooms? | Plan your visit |
| The class is full. Is there a waiting list? | the trial request doubles as a waiting-list message when the class is marked full |
| What happens at the first class, and will she be all right without me? | the first-class steps on the trial page |
| Everything in one place for the year (rules, collection, photo consent)? | BAL-7 parent handbook (Signature) |
