# Restaurants: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `restaurant`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## The plan (from the original trade list)

2. RESTAURANTS AND CAFÉS. Built 27 Oct 2026: kit `restaurant`, look `carte`, demo "Saffron & Ash" (fictional
   charcoal grill, Northern Quarter, Manchester). Plan split agreed with the owner (be generous below Signature):
   - Starter: `menu` (the sitting on now, diet filters, the 14 allergens on every dish, kitchen-closes line),
     `specials` (today's specials and sold-out from a Sheet), `table-request` (WhatsApp), `delivery` (app links).
   - Plus: `menu-page` (every sitting, drinks, allergen chart), `tonight` (live walk-in tables, from the Sheet),
     `order` (takeaway tray with total and allergens, WhatsApp), `pairings`, `events`, `private-dining`.
   - Signature: `the-table` (group diet planner), `the-room` (dining room plan, pick where to sit),
     `the-pass` (the plate up close with what's on it).
   The Carte look turns to its evening colours for an evening sitting (day/evening per sitting in `menu.sittings`).
