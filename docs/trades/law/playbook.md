# Law firms (private client): playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `law`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Law firm, private client (kit `law`, look `portland`, demo Calder Wyke) — merged 2 Nov 2026 (#36)
- **Design process:** three directions, one approved screen revised three times (the hero moved inside to a panelled
  library; the white stucco house moved to discreet arrival), the mini design system in `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/law-firm.md`, real
  photography in one stone grade, the blind-embossed initials seal as the brand mark. Handover `sitefloor-internal/docs/HANDOVER-LAW.md`.
- **Always on (the SRA requires them):** regulation (the SRA badge under the first call to action, the SRA number on
  every page, the plate) and complaints; prices and service information whenever the firm offers a service rule 1 lists.
- **Starter:** practice index, people with SRA IDs, Speak to a partner by practice, the free prices, complaints and Plan
  your visit pages, words we use (glossary).
- **Plus:** practice page (a photograph per area, facts, the matter step by step, the will reminder), people page,
  insights (photographed), conflict-safe first contact, discreet arrival, our day in yours, when someone has died, what
  to gather, the will review reminder.
- **Signature:** a page per practice area, public matters (client-approved, publicly sourced), guides for families
  (photographed, reviewed by a named solicitor), the probate cost estimator, if there's no will, the house room by room,
  the first-meeting pack (details live in the link), your matter by code, the succession map (an illustration).
- **Alternatives (not built):** a private reading room for existing clients, a City/corporate look on the same kit
  (glass, atrium, tower views; planned).
- **Honesty:** SRA numbers are 000000 on demos and real on clients; prices, people, matters and insurance only from the
  firm; no outcomes, success rates or reviews; insights and guides marked general information, not advice; regulator
  and intestacy facts dated in `src/kits/law/lib/`; the matter sheet is refused if it holds anything beyond code, stage
  and time.
- **Setting up a client:** the firm's SRA number and badge frame address, legal name and entity, insurer and cover, a
  4:5 photograph of their own room, a photograph per practice area (16:10) and of the house, their practice areas and who
  leads each, every solicitor's SRA ID, their prices for any rule 1 service (with VAT, disbursements, stages, team), their
  complaints procedure and contact, and the email that receives first contact. Neutral portraits or none. Running cost £0.
