# Lingerie boutiques: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `lingerie`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Luxury lingerie boutique (new `lingerie` kit) — Marese built 29 Oct 2026 (look `marese`, demo `sites/demos/lingerie`, handover `sitefloor-internal/docs/HANDOVER-LINGERIE.md`)
- **Questions:** `docs/trades/lingerie/questions.md`. **Directions, mockups and the plan split:**
  `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/lingerie-directions.md`.
- **Brief (owner, second round):** a Mayfair/Knightsbridge/Chelsea boutique selling £400 sets as desire: campaign
  photography of women wearing the pieces (low light, confident, a hotel suite at night), sensual but no nudity;
  old money, seductive, prices visible, a pale ground with one deep contrast
  (oxblood); one moody campaign grade; full-bleed hero and close crops; guide pages per `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/lingerie-directions.md`. Casting: model-look women, no visible tattoos or
  piercings, the lingerie as the focus. Fabric close-ups only as support. Never novelty or pink-and-black.
- **Honesty:** never state returns law beyond the sources in the questions table; "discreet billing" only in the
  shop's own words; no brand logos; demo prices marked as demo.
