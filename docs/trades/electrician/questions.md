# Electricians: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `electrician`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Electrician (electrician kit, look `bluehour`, demo Corbel Light & Power)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Are you registered (NICEIC / NAPIT)? Can I check? | `registers`: only the client's schemes, each linked to its public register (validated by host) |
| Will it be notified, do I get the certificates? Where are mine? | `circuits` (certificates line); `certificates`: the client's certificates by code, with the next EICR and a calendar reminder |
| How much for lighting, a rewire, a charger? | `room-designer` and `estimate` (ranges, rounded out) and `ev-check`, all from the client's own prices |
| Can one keypad do evening, cinema, away? | `scene-keypad` (the client's photographs per scene); `house-3d` (Signature) |
| Why is each light where it is? | `light-reasons` (numbered lights on a real photograph) |
| Will a charger work on my drive and supply? Grants? | `ev-check` (standard / survey / no, never a false yes; grant link to gov.uk only); `ev-3d` |
| What happens and how long? What should I have ready? | `project-steps`, `before-you-call` |
| It keeps tripping / burning smell: what now? | `fault-guide`: what to tell the electrician and to keep clear; never a repair step (validation refuses repair wording) |
| Can you come today? | `call-out` (the client's hours and terms) |
| Landlord: how often an EICR? | `certificates` landlord line, from gov.uk with the date checked |
