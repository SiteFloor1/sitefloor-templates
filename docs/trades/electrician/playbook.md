# Electricians: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `electrician`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Electrician and smart-home installer (new `electrician` kit, look `bluehour`, demo Corbel Light & Power) — built 2 Nov 2026
- **Design process:** three mockups, direction A (Blue hour) approved with changes; the contract is
  `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/electrician.md`. Handover: `sitefloor-internal/docs/HANDOVER-ELECTRICIAN.md`.
- **Starter:** the circuit chart of services with registers (each linked to its public register), the call-out line,
  lit rooms, the fault guide (what to tell the electrician, never a repair step), questions, visit.
- **Plus:** why each light is there (numbered lights on a photograph), the room designer, the dimmer over two of the
  client's photographs, the project steps, before you call, the projects page, the EV check, your-project page.
- **Signature:** the scene keypad, the whole-house estimate, the house in 3D (a full-width chapter: a still first, loads on tap,
  lights up room by room, prices the lit rooms and the garden), the EV charger in 3D (drag the charger along the wall, the lead is measured), certificates by code with the
  next EICR and a calendar reminder (an add-on on lower plans; the client's Google Sheet).
- **Alternatives (not built this round):** the client's own floor plan drawn as the 3D house; a before/after rewire
  slider; a "what's on this circuit" board map from the client's schedule of test results; an EV tariff calculator
  (needs a free, licensed tariff source first).
- **Honesty:** registers, insurance, guarantees and certificate intervals only from the client; the landlord rule only
  from gov.uk with the date checked; EV grants linked to gov.uk, never an amount; demo registers labelled "(demo)".
- **Setting up a client:** a dusk photograph of a finished house per scene (same viewpoint ideally), three or four lit
  interiors, a pair of photographs of one room at low and full light (same viewpoint, same size), their prices
  (fittings, works, EV install and cable per metre), their schemes and register links, their fault-guide wording,
  their certificate sheet (code, kind, issued, next_due, note). Running cost £0.

## The plan (from the original trade list)

7. ELECTRICIANS (and other trades such as plumbers later). Demo: fictional
   Customers ask: Are you registered and insured? Can you come today? How much? Which areas?
   Do I get a certificate? Is the work guaranteed?
   Core: accreditations (real ones only, with their check links); service area by postcode;
   "from" prices; questions; reviews.
   Plus: one page per service (rewires, EV chargers, EICRs); a landlord page.
   SIGNATURE, "Design the lighting":
   - a drawn room (kitchen or living room) with day and night modes;
   - switch on downlights, pendants, LED strips and sockets;
   - a live estimate and "send for a quote".
   Extras:
   - "Is it an emergency?" triage: what to do now and when to call;
   - a landlord EICR checker: enter the last test date to see when the next is due
     (5-year rule in England);
   - an EV charger estimate.
