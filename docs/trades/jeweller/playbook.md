# Fine jewellers: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `jeweller`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Fine jeweller (jeweller kit, look `nacre`, demo Aldous Marr) — built 29 Oct 2026
- **Design process:** three directions, one approved screen (home top and a collection), the mini design system in
  `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/aldous-marr.md` (the contract), real photography with one grade, a monogram as the brand mark.
- **Starter:** every piece shown large with only the client's facts and prices ("Price on request" and "From £…" only
  when set); collections; one piece at a time; the private appointment request on WhatsApp; drop a hint; the room; in
  the box; what happens at an appointment; questions, reviews, seasons.
- **Plus:** a page for every piece (one page however many), bridal, the engagement guide, wedding bands, bespoke (the
  client's process and timings, deposit by Payment Link, a brief), family pieces, gifts by price band, occasions,
  birthstones, ring size (string, a ring on a calibrated screen, a sizer by post), aftercare with a yearly reminder; the
  tray; anniversary reminders; the stone guide; gift cards.
- **Signature:** the piece in detail (macro photographs on piece pages),
  on a hand at true size, pair it, from sketch to ring, inside the band, planning a proposal.
- **Waiting:** the appointment with the shared date picker (the days and times are worked out in
  `src/kits/jeweller/lib/calendar.js`; the picker comes with the car hire PR).
- **Alternatives (not built this round):** daylight and candlelight (the same piece in two of the client's photographs:
  needs a real pair, stock can't supply one), the same design in each metal (needs a matched set), a vitrine opened a
  drawer at a time, the client's own box opening on the chosen piece.
- **Honesty:** hallmarks, assay marks, certificates and lab names, carat, purity, sourcing ("ethical", "conflict-free",
  "recycled", "lab-grown"), warranties and prices come only from the client, per piece, in the matching fact field.
- **Setting up a client:** a 4:5 photograph of every piece (and a 900×1125 card copy), its facts and price as they want
  them shown, collections with a photograph of a piece worn for each door, a 16:9 and a 3:4 crop of the hero, the room,
  the box, their steps for an appointment and a commission, their deposit Payment Link, a hand photograph with a ruler
  beside it (for true size), macro photographs (Signature). Their signature colour drives the box band and the
  appointment. Running cost £0.
