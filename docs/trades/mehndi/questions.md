# Mehndi and henna artists: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `mehndi`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Bridal and event mehndi artist (mehndi kit, branch `trade/mehndi`)
Researched 6 Nov 2026 from real UK artists' supplier listings (a London artist who travels nationwide, a Leeds
bridal specialist, an artist with a 20-mile call-out rule), UK wedding-supplier directories and their booking
forms, a UK South Asian bridal magazine's advice on timing, and the NHS page on PPD. Reviews on these listings were
few, so the worries below come mostly from what artists answer in their FAQs and booking forms. Every price, time,
travel rule and policy on a client site comes from the client.

| Customers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Are you free on my date (and my mehndi night)? | date check (MEHN-2): open, a few left, or taken, from the artist's Sheet; "ask about this date" on WhatsApp with the date in it. Never "booked" |
| How far ahead should I book? | date check line in the artist's words ("brides book 6–12 months ahead in summer"); waiting list when a date is taken |
| What does bridal mehndi cost? What's the difference between packages? | packages (MEHN-1): bridal by coverage (hands to wrist, mid-arm, elbow; feet to ankle or shin), what's included (trial, aftercare kit, touch-up), from prices |
| Do you do the bride's family and guests too? How much for 20 guests? | guest mehndi: price per guest or per hour, designs per hour, how many artists; guest quote (MEHN-6) |
| How long will it take? When should I book the slot? | packages show hours per coverage; wedding week planner (MEHN-5) works back from the wedding |
| When should I have it done so it's darkest on the day? | stain guide (MEHN-4): the stain darkens over about two days; planner suggests the mehndi day; the artist's own timing overrides |
| Why is it orange? Will it go dark? How long does it last? | stain guide: paste to orange to deep brown, roughly how many days it lasts, why palms go darker than arms |
| How do I look after it? Can I wash my hands? | aftercare (MEHN-4): keep the paste on, avoid water, warmth, no scrubbing; printable card and a link sent on WhatsApp |
| Is your henna natural? What's in it? | natural henna page: what the artist's paste is made from (their own list), no PPD, "if it stains black in an hour it isn't henna" |
| Is black henna safe? | the PPD warning with the NHS source and date checked (below) |
| Can I do a patch test? I have sensitive skin. | natural henna page and booking: patch test offer, in the artist's words |
| Do you travel to the venue or my home? Is there a travel fee? | travel by postcode district: free area, then the artist's fee; venue and home bookings; studio address if they have one |
| What do I need to do before you arrive (nails, waxing, clean dry skin, a table, good light)? | "before I arrive" card on the packages page and in the planner |
| Can you put our names, our wedding date or a meaningful symbol in the design? | personal details request (MEHN-7): names, initials, date, a place, a pet, a prayer; where to hide them; reference photos by WhatsApp |
| Can I see more of your work? Do you do Arabic, Indo-Arabic, Moroccan, minimal? | design library (MEHN-3) by style, coverage and hours, photographed close up |
| Do you do Eid, Karva Chauth, Diwali, baby showers, hen parties, corporate stalls? | events and Eid page with dates from the artist's Sheet; corporate as an enquiry |
| What deposit do you take, and is it refundable? | booking terms in the artist's words (deposit, balance date, cancellation); the artist's Stripe link if they use one |
| Are you insured? | public liability line if the client provides it (several UK artists state £5m cover) |
| Do you offer jagua or white henna? | listed only if the artist offers it, with what it is |

### Black henna (PPD): what the site says
Source: NHS, [Hair dye reactions](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions/), page last reviewed 29 December
2025, checked 6 November 2026. The NHS says "black henna" temporary tattoos contain high levels of PPD, which can
raise the risk of an allergic reaction to products containing PPD later, including hair dye, and advises avoiding
PPD products, especially if you have had a black henna tattoo. It points to the British Skin Foundation for more.

On a client site: one plain paragraph on the natural henna page and a line in aftercare, linking the NHS page and
showing the date it was checked. No scare imagery, no medical advice beyond what the NHS page says.
