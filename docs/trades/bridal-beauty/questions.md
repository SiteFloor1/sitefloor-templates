# Bridal hair and makeup artists: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `bridalbeauty`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Bridal hair and makeup artist (bridalbeauty kit, branch `trade/bridal-beauty`)
Researched 15 Nov 2026 from real UK artists' FAQ and price pages (solo artists in Hertfordshire, Birmingham, south
London and Glasgow; a London agency with artists in several cities; London Asian bridal specialists), their booking
terms on booking apps, UK wedding-supplier directories (listings, price guides, "questions to ask your artist"
articles) and their reviews, a UK legal-services guide to makeup artists' booking terms, and UK bridal magazines'
skin prep advice. Reviews mostly praise how long the look lasted and a calm morning; the worries are the start time,
running late and what is included. Every price, minute, fee, area and policy on a client site comes from the client.

| Customers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Are you free on my date? | date check (HMUA-2): open, a few left or taken from the artist's Sheet; "ask about this date" on WhatsApp with the date in it. Never "booked" |
| How far ahead should I book? | date check line in the artist's words (artists say 10–18 months for May–October Saturdays); waiting list when taken |
| What does it cost for me? What's included (trial, lashes, touch-up kit)? | packages (HMUA-1): bride hair and makeup, hair or makeup alone, trial in or out, what's included, from prices |
| How much for my bridesmaids, mum, flower girls? | party prices per person by service; party quote (Signature) totals it as a WhatsApp message |
| Do I have to have a minimum party on a Saturday? | packages: the artist's peak-date minimum, if they have one, in their words |
| What time will we have to start? Is there an early-start fee? | "what time do we start?" (Plus) and the wedding morning (HMUA-6): worked back from the ceremony, the artist's fee named when the start is before their cut-off |
| How long does each person take? Can you fit everyone in? | the morning: the artist's minutes per service; a second artist suggested when it won't fit, at the artist's own rate |
| Will you stay for touch-ups or come back for the evening? | packages: stay-on per hour and evening restyle, if offered |
| When should I have my trial? Where? Can someone come with me? | trials page and trial request (HMUA-3): the artist's window (many say 3–6 weeks to 6 months before), where trials happen, guests welcome or not |
| Can my bridesmaids or mum have a trial too? | trials page: party trials, price, whether on the same day |
| What should I bring to my trial? | trials page: photos you like, the dress and veil photos, a top like you'll wear, clean skin |
| Do you travel? Is there a travel or London charge? | travel by postcode (HMUA-5): free area, then the artist's per-mile or flat fee, for the trial and the morning separately; parking and congestion charge lines if the artist charges them |
| Do you do destination weddings? | destination page: travel days, flights and accommodation as named lines, quoted |
| What deposit secures my date? Is it refundable? When is the balance due? | booking terms in the artist's words (booking fee, balance date, cancellation, date changes); the artist's Stripe link if used |
| How do I prepare my skin? Should I have a facial? | skin prep (HMUA-7): dated milestones in the artist's words (no new products in the last weeks, last facial well before, brows a week or two before); clinical treatments only as "ask your clinician" |
| I have sensitive skin or allergies (lash glue, products) | booking form asks; patch test offer in the artist's words; never a medical claim |
| Do you do Asian bridal, dupatta and jewellery setting, hijab styling? Several events? | Asian bridal page: services and minutes per event, the artist's own work shown, each event's trial |
| Do you work with my hair type, texture or extensions? | look library by hair type and finish; extensions fitting if offered (client supplies) |
| Will it last all day and photograph well? | look library and real reviews (copied from Google with name and date); touch-up kit if included |
| Can I see brides like me? | portfolio (look library) by skin tone, finish and hair, the artist's own work only, with consent |
| Do you do men, grooms, mothers, children? | packages: who the artist does, child prices |
| Are you insured? Qualified? | public liability and training lines only if the client provides them |
| What do we need to have ready on the morning? | "before I arrive" card: light, a table and chair by a window, power, a button-front top, the timings |

### What the site never says
No guaranteed longevity, "waterproof", "flawless" or "perfect" claims; no medical or skin-treatment advice beyond "ask
your clinician"; no invented reviews or "brides served" counts; no faces of real brides without recorded consent.
