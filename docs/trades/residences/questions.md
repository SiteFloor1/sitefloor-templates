# Luxury residences and hotel-grade rentals: customer questions

What guests and residents ask a residences operator in London before they get in touch: a single building (or a few)
of furnished homes let by the night, the month or on a tenancy, with hotel-grade service. Drawn from the FAQ, "stay with
us", "residences" and enquiry pages of London serviced-apartment and branded-residence operators, corporate-relocation
briefs, and the themes that recur in their public reviews (the final bill being higher than the price shown, what
"serviced" really includes, noise, the view not matching the photos, check-in at night). Rules are from GOV.UK and
legislation.gov.uk; the tenancy rules were checked for the letting trade on 15 Nov 2026 and are re-checked, with the
date shown, before the build. England only.

## Guests and residents

| # | Question | What the site must answer |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Which homes are free for my dates, and for how long? | Homes from the operator's sheet with the dates each is free from; nothing shown as free once the sheet is stale |
| R2 | What will I pay in total for my stay, every fee included? | One total for the length chosen: nightly or monthly rate plus every mandatory charge (cleaning, service, VAT where charged) in the headline price, never added later (Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, CMA drip-pricing guidance) |
| R3 | Is a long stay a tenancy or a hotel-style stay, and what changes? | Short stays: the operator's booking terms. Tenancies: only the permitted payments (rent, holding deposit up to 1 week, tenancy deposit up to 5 or 6 weeks), protected deposit, the tenancy rules from GOV.UK, dated. Which applies is set by the operator, never guessed by the site |
| R4 | What does "serviced" include? How often is it cleaned, are linens and towels changed? | The operator's own service list per stay length, side by side |
| R5 | What is the home actually like: size, beds, bath, the view, which floor, light? | Size in sq ft and m², bedrooms and bathrooms, floor, which way it faces, real photos of that home, floor plan |
| R6 | How quiet is it? Is there air conditioning, blackout, a proper desk, fast Wi-Fi? | The operator's own facts per home (glazing, cooling, blackout, desk, broadband speed if they supply it) |
| R7 | How do I arrive? Late check-in, a car from the airport, luggage, keys? | Arrival steps and the concierge's hours; transfers and extras only as the operator lists them, with their prices |
| R8 | Can the concierge get groceries in, book a table, arrange a cot, a cleaner, a car? | The concierge's actual services, with any price, and a pre-filled WhatsApp to ask |
| R9 | What's nearby: the walk to the Tube, Hyde Park, a gym, a good supermarket, schools? | The neighbourhood map: places the operator chooses, with walking minutes the operator supplies or the map computes, never ratings |
| R10 | Can I see it first? In person, or on a video call? | Viewing request or the operator's open slots; video viewings if offered; the client's own 3D tour if they have one |
| R11 | Can my company pay? Do you do corporate lets and relocation packs? | Corporate terms and invoicing as the operator sets them; a relocation request |
| R12 | Pets, children, cots, accessible homes, a lift? | Per home, as the operator supplies; pet requests on tenancies considered (Renters' Rights Act 2025) |
| R13 | What are the cancellation and payment terms? When do I pay? | The operator's booking terms, in plain words, before the enquiry; deposits by the operator's own Stripe link |
| R14 | Who protects my money and who do I complain to? | For letting work: the redress scheme, client money protection and the fees list, as the client supplies them, linked to GOV.UK with the date checked (a legal duty for letting agents) |

## Owners (only if the operator also manages other owners' homes)

| # | Question | What the site must answer |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | What do you charge, with VAT? | Each service's fee in one list (Consumer Rights Act 2015 s83 for letting agents) |
| O2 | What do you do, and what could my home earn? | The operator's service list; earnings only as an illustration the operator approves, never a promise |

## Rules to check at build (sources, not advice)
- Headline prices include every mandatory fee: CMA, DMCC Act 2024 (in force 6 Apr 2025).
- Tenant Fees Act 2019 permitted payments; deposit caps (5 weeks under £50,000 a year, 6 weeks from £50,000).
- Renters' Rights Act 2025 (assured periodic tenancies from 1 May 2026); tenancies above £100,000 a year and company lets
  are not assured tenancies (Housing Act 1988 Sch 1): the card says what the operator says, never a guess.
- Greater London short lets: the 90-nights-a-year limit for homes without planning permission for short-let use
  (Deregulation Act 2015 s44); serviced-apartment buildings may hold a different use class. The operator states which.
- Letting agents: redress scheme, client money protection, published fees (Consumer Rights Act 2015, Client Money
  Protection Schemes for Property Agents Regulations 2019).
