# Garden design and landscaping: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `garden`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

From UK garden designers' own FAQ pages (independent designers in the North West and the South, a designer-builder in the
Midlands), a national trade directory's hiring advice, landscapers' public review pages on a trade directory, and GOV.UK
planning guidance, Nov 2026. Feature codes are the approved LAND list in [`sitefloor-internal/docs/VISION-REFERENCES.md`](https://github.com/SiteFloor1/sitefloor-internal/blob/main/docs/VISION-REFERENCES.md).

## Before they call: what do you actually do?
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Do you design, build, or both? Can I just have the design and use my own builder? | the "design / build / care" split on the home page and its three pages (LAND pages 3, 4, 6) |
| What's the difference between a garden designer, a landscaper and a gardener? | the same split, in one plain line each |
| What does a design include? Concept, master plan, planting plan, construction details? | Design process (LAND-6): each stage, what you receive, when you can stop |
| I only want a planting plan. Can you do that remotely? | Design page: the client's own options (planting plan only, remote) |
| What kind of gardens do you do? Is mine too small or too big? | Gardens by style and size (LAND-1): courtyards, roof terraces, family gardens, country estates |
| Can I see gardens like mine, before and after? | Gardens (LAND-1), before/after (LAND-2) |
| Are you qualified, accredited, insured? | the client's own memberships and accreditations (only those they hold), with links to check |

## Time
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| How long does the design take? And the build? | Design process (LAND-6): the client's own typical weeks per stage, "depends on" factors |
| When should I contact you if I'm building an extension first? | Design process: "as early as possible", in the client's words |
| When do you have space to start? How far ahead are you booked? | a "next design start" and "next build start" line from the client's sheet (fails safe: hidden if out of date) |
| Do you work all year? What happens in winter or wet weather? | Garden calendar (LAND-3): design all year, build when ground allows, planting windows, maintenance months |
| When is the best time to plant? Can I have it ready for summer? | Garden calendar (LAND-3), "start by" dates worked back from a target month |
| How long until the planting looks established? | Planting palette (LAND-4), a garden through the year (LAND-5): plant sizes at planting and how a bed fills in |

## Planning rules (GOV.UK only, dated, "a guide, not a decision")
England only, from GOV.UK's householder permitted development technical guidance and the 2015 order on legislation.gov.uk,
both checked 5 Nov 2026. Shown on site as a short guide with the date checked, the source links and "a guide, not a
decision: check with your council, especially for a listed building, a flat, a conservation area or an Article 4 area".
Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own rules; a site outside England shows its own nation's source or none.
| Question | What GOV.UK says (summary) |
|---|---|
| Do I need permission for a new fence or wall? | Usually not up to 2 m high; 1 m next to a road used by vehicles; not around a listed building |
| Do I need permission for decking? | Usually not if no more than 0.3 m above the ground (anything higher counts as a raised platform), with the 50% cover limit and not in front of the house |
| A garden room, studio or shed? | Usually not if single storey, behind the front of the house, max 2.5 m high within 2 m of a boundary (otherwise 3 m, or 4 m with a dual-pitched roof, eaves 2.5 m), all buildings under half the garden, and not used as a bedroom, bathroom or kitchen |
| Paving the front garden? | Over 5 m² needs to be permeable or drain to a permeable area |
| Will you handle any application for me? | the client's own answer (many designers do for their own schemes) |

## On site and after
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| How do you get materials in if there's no side access? Will you go through the house? | questions (the client's own approach to access, skips, protection) |
| Will you leave it tidy? What about neighbours, noise, parking? | questions; reviews (tidiness and communication are the most praised points in public reviews) |
| Who looks after it afterwards? Do you do maintenance? How often? | Maintenance plans (LAND-7): visits per year, what each visit does, by season |
| Low maintenance: what can I have that doesn't need much care? | Gardens by style; planting palette (LAND-4) with a care level per plant |
| Where will it be sunny? What will grow where? | Where the sun falls (LAND-8): the garden's sun and shade by month and hour |
| Do you guarantee the planting? | questions (the client's own plant guarantee, if any) |
| Do you cover my area? | service areas (postcode districts, shared checker) |
