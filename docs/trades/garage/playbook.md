# Garage and parking rentals: playbook

What to build for this trade (no kit yet). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## The plan (from the original trade list)

3. GARAGE AND DRIVEWAY RENTALS (storage, parking, workshops). Demo: fictional
   Customers ask: What size, and what fits? Is it free now? Price per week or month?
   Secure? 24/7 access? Power? Minimum term?
   Core: units list with live availability from a Sheet; prices; security and access
   questions; Plan your visit.
   Plus: one page per site or location; a "how renting works" page; reserve with a deposit
   (Stripe Payment Link).
   SIGNATURE, "Pick your space":
   - a drawn site plan with each unit coloured free, reserved or let;
   - tap a unit for its size;
   - a "what fits?" check: pick a car, van, motorbike or number of boxes and the unit
     shows what fits, drawn to scale;
   - a monthly price and "reserve on WhatsApp".
   Extras: a waiting-list form for when it's full.
