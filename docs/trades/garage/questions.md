# Garage and parking rentals: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (no kit yet). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

Gathered 16 Nov 2026 from: listings for UK parking spaces, lock-up garages and secure car storage on the two biggest
UK peer-to-peer storage and parking marketplaces and a property portal (London, Home Counties, Norfolk, Hampshire);
a national lock-up operator's standard terms; council garage tenancy handbooks and "rent a garage" pages (St Albans,
Cambridge, Adur and Worthing, Folkestone and Hythe); multi-storey operators' monthly-permit listings (height limits,
weekday-only access); and private car-storage facilities in London and the regions (indoor storage tiers, battery
conditioners, covers, 48-hour access, private consultations). Ranked by how often the answer decides the sale.

What decides it most: price and whether a space is free now, then "will my car actually fit" (height limits of
1.90–2.10 m in underground and multi-storey sites catch out SUVs, vans and roof boxes), then how safe and how easy
to get in after dark. What customers complain about most: a fob or key that takes days to arrive, someone else in
"my" bay, a ramp or turn that's too tight for the car, notice periods tied to quarter days, and no reply after paying.
