# Construction and building firms: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `construction`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## High-end builders (kit `construction`, look `handover`, demo Pellow & Garth) — built 4 Nov 2026
- **Design process:** three directions, Handover chosen and revised once (neutral ground, the visualiser as the
  first-screen wow, how a build runs as a section through a house); mini design system and references in
  `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/construction.md`. Handover `sitefloor-internal/docs/HANDOVER-CONSTRUCTION.md`.
- **Starter:** the extension visualiser in 3D (photo first, loads on tap), how a build runs with "if we started in…" (the
  handover months from the builder's own stage lengths: the easy extra), recently handed over, what we build,
  accreditations and insurance, Start a project, Plan your visit.
- **Plus:** projects with a page for each (before, during and after, with the client's written consent) and the map of
  districts (OpenStreetMap on tap), services, how we build, the Start a project page, the specification library with the
  sample box (the easy extra: tick finishes, sent on WhatsApp), what it costs (no figures without the builder's rates),
  planning and party walls (GOV.UK, dated), reviews, what's worth checking (the house's age: lead pipes, asbestos,
  wall ties, earlier sign-offs; general guidance with official sources).
- **Signature (the showpieces):** the visualiser page with the **planning guide** (the depth against GOV.UK's
  permitted-development limits for the house type, drawn on the model, "a guide, not a decision") and the **light study**
  (the real sun for the month, time and postcode district, the garden's direction, a compass dial); your house, this
  week by site code; the build in 3D, stage by stage; living through a build (dates from a start, what home is like,
  a calendar file); a page per kind of work with planning at a glance; warranties and insurance; the team; the journal; **your loft in
  3D** (a rear dormer, a hip-to-gable or both, the volume against the 40/50 m³ Class B allowance, dated); **for our
  neighbours** (a page per live site by district: hours, contact, this week from the builder's sheet).
  Demo: 35 pages built, 19 counted with the home page (a collection counts once; unlisted pages, Plan your visit and privacy don't count).
- **Honesty rules built in:** no prices without the builder's rates (a demo may carry none); accreditation numbers all
  noughts on a demo; a project's "before" photo only with the client's written consent; a district, never an address;
  journal sources only from gov.uk with the date checked.
- **Next ideas (not built):** a side-return extension on the same model (half the house's width, against the side
  rules); the party wall notice drafted from the visitor's choices (the neighbours' districts, the work, the dates) as a
  letter the builder checks; a "snagging" list at handover the client ticks on their phone, sent back on WhatsApp.
