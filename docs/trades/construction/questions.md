# Construction and building firms: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `construction`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## High-end builders: extensions, basements, renovations, new builds, listed buildings (construction kit)
Gathered from what real UK design-and-build firms answer and what homeowner guides tell people to ask (sources in
`sitefloor-internal/docs/design/construction.md`). Every fact (projects, stage lengths, accreditations, insurance, rates) comes from the
builder's `site.json`. Planning and building-regulation facts appear only in the journal, linked to GOV.UK with the date
each page was checked. Nothing is a "typical" price.

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What would an extension look like on my house, and how big would it be? | the extension visualiser on the first screen (Starter): house type, depth, roof, before and after, with the size and new floor area worked out |
| Have you built something like mine, near me? | recently handed over (Starter); projects with a page for each and the map of districts (Plus); each kind of work with its projects (Signature) |
| What will it cost? | what it costs (Plus): how the builder prices, what decides it, what's paid to others, VAT; figures only from the builder's own £ per m² rates (a table there, and a range on the visualiser page), "a range, not a quote"; with no rates, no figure anywhere |
| Will my extension need planning permission? | the planning guide on the 3D (Signature): the depth against GOV.UK's limits for the house type; planning and party walls (Plus); planning at a glance on each service page; always "a guide, not a decision" |
| Will the new room get the sun? | the light study on the 3D (Signature): the real sun for the month, time, district and garden direction |
| What will our home be like during the work? When would it be finished? | living through a build (Signature): each stage's dates from a start date, what home is like, the noisy part, a calendar file; "if we started in…" on the home page (Starter) |
| Can we convert our loft without planning permission? | your loft in 3D (Signature): the volume a dormer or hip-to-gable adds against GOV.UK's allowance; "a guide, not a decision" |
| What should we check before we start? Old pipes, asbestos? | what's worth checking (Plus), by the house's age, facts linked to their official source |
| (Neighbours) When do you work? Who do I call? What's happening this week? | for our neighbours (Signature): a page per site, by district |
| Can I see and touch the materials? | the sample box on the specification (Plus) |
| What do other clients say? | reviews (Plus): the builder's own Google reviews |
| How long will it take, stage by stage? | how a build runs (Starter); how we build with the builder's own stage lengths (Plus); the build in 3D (Signature) |
| Are you insured? Members of anything? | accreditations and insurance from the builder's certificates, with the date checked and the public register (Starter; a page on Signature) |
| Who runs my job, and how will I know what's happening? | how a build runs (one project manager, the weekly update); your house, this week by site code (Signature) |
| What about the neighbours? | planning and party walls (Plus): party wall notices, the 14 days, what happens if they don't reply, from GOV.UK |
| Can we stay at home during the work? | how we build (living at home during the work, in the builder's words); the journal |
| How do payments work? Is there a contract and a guarantee? | how we build (contract, payments, defects period, in the builder's words) |
| What materials do you use? | the specification library (Plus) |
| How do I start? | Start a project (Starter): what, the postcode district only, when, sent on WhatsApp; a page with what happens next (Plus) |
