# Car hire: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `hire`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Car hire (hire kit, trade/car-hire)
Performance and luxury hire for weekends, weddings and events. Every rule below comes from the client's `site.json`;
the site never supplies a default, a "typical" figure or a legal answer, and never says a car is booked.

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What does it cost for these dates, all in? | the date-range quote (Plus): day, weekend or week rate, delivery, extra miles and deposit, each part shown, from the client's price table; "from" prices per day on the fleet (Starter) |
| Is this car free that weekend? | "free on the sheet" from the client's availability Sheet (Starter); days blocked in the date picker (Plus). Never a guarantee; an unreadable Sheet says "ask us" |
| What deposit do you take, and when do I get it back? | each car's deposit and the client's return wording on the car (Starter); the deposit button (Plus), which holds a request, not a booking |
| How old do I need to be? How long must I have held a licence? Do points matter? | each car's age, licence-years and points rules (Starter); the driver-requirements checker (Plus): "you can request this car if…", never a decision |
| What does the insurance cover, what's the excess, and can I reduce it? | the client's insurance summary and excess per car, and any excess-reduction option they sell (Starter) |
| Is there a mileage limit? What does each extra mile cost? | each car's included miles and extra-mile price (Starter); extra miles added to the quote (Plus) |
| Do you deliver and collect, or do I come to you? Where? To my home? To the airport? | handover location and hours; delivery to home and to named airports, each with the client's own price and radius (Starter); delivery in the quote (Plus) |
| Do you have a chauffeur? What does it cost by the hour or the day? | the chauffeur class (Rolls-Royce, Maybach) with the client's own rates and minimum hours (Plus) |
| Which car is right for a wedding, a weekend away, a family, a track day? | the fleet in classes (everyday luxury, performance, SUV, supercar, chauffeur), each car's seats, boot and rules (Starter) |
| What exactly is the car: trim, interior, spec? | each car's page: trim name, interior, the client's own spec figures (Plus) |
| Can I have it for a wedding? Ribbons? A chauffeur? | the wedding and event page (Plus): only what the client offers, with their prices |
| What's the fuel or charging policy? | each car's fuel or charge rule from the client (Starter) |
| What do I bring on the day? | the client's handover checklist (licence, proof of address, the card for the deposit), on the handover section (Starter) |
| Can a second person drive? | the client's additional-driver rule and price (Starter); added to the quote (Plus) |
| What happens if I'm late back? | the client's late-return rule (Starter) |
| Can I give a drive as a present? | gift vouchers (Plus), via the client's own Payment Links |
| How do I ask? | a pre-filled WhatsApp request with car, dates and quote (Starter and up) |
