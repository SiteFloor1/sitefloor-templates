# Grand cafés and tea rooms: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `restaurant`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Grand café (restaurant kit, look `salon`, demo Grenat) — built 28 Oct 2026
- **Design process:** one screen approved before the build (the top of the home page and afternoon tea); real
  photography with one grade; a brand world (monogram under the pomegranate's crown, the crown as a band); references
  and the mini design system in `src/looks/salon/README.md`.
- **Starter:** the sittings with open now; the carte with every allergen and the printed carte; today's specials and
  sold-out from the sheet; the table request as a concierge card; day to candlelight (in the look on every plan).
- **Plus:** cakes to order (the earliest day after each cake's notice, a fixed deposit by Stripe link over a threshold);
  private hire; live music now; the dress code card; seasonal stands; candlelight tonight (walk-in tables from the
  sheet); gift vouchers (shared); pages for afternoon tea, gifts, What's on and Plan your visit.
- **Signature:** Your table (the showpiece: the stand set guest by guest from the kitchen's own photos, every piece that
  isn't right marked and made differently, place cards, the champagne table, the occasion and the words for the plate,
  sent with the table request); the pâtisserie window and the house box (Stripe link per box); the salon plan; Give a
  sitting (the card set as you write, the shop's Stripe link, the names on WhatsApp); the tea sommelier.
- **Alternatives (not built):** the cake atelier (the Signature version of cakes to order); the pianist's requests
  (ask for a piece by WhatsApp while the piano plays).
- **Setting up a client:** the tea menu with diet marks and allergens; a photo of each tier and, optionally, a plate; whether
  the kitchen makes swaps; the tea library in the shop's own words; one Stripe Payment Link per gift and one for the cake
  deposit; one per box size; a photo of each window piece and one wide photo of the room; the seasonal teas' dates and
  prices; the music times; the dress code in the shop's words. Running cost £0.
