# Grand cafés and tea rooms: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `restaurant`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Grand café and tea room
- Can I book afternoon tea, and when are the sittings? (the sittings with open now; the concierge card)
- What's on the stand, and can you make it vegan, gluten-free or without nuts for one of us? (Your table)
- Can you put a message on the plate for a birthday? (Your table's occasion)
- Which tea should I have? Is there anything without caffeine? (the tea sommelier; the library)
- How much is it, and with champagne? (the price line)
- Is there a dress code? Can I bring children or a dog? (the dress code card)
- Is there live music, and when? (music now)
- Can I give afternoon tea as a present? (Give a sitting)
- Can I order a whole cake, and how much notice do you need? (cakes to order)
- Do you have a private room? (private hire)
- What's sold out today? (the sheet)
