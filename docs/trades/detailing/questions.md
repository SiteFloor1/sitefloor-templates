# Car detailing: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `detailing`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Premium car detailing (detailing kit, branch `trade/detailing`)
Researched 2 Nov 2026 from real UK studios (Konings, Winchester: supercar and classic detailing and XPEL film; GVE
London, Uxbridge: a detailing centre with film cut in-house) and UK owners' forums (911UK, Taycan Forum, MBClub).
Every price, length of job, brand, warranty and care step on a client site comes from the client.

| Customers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| How much for my car? | `packages` (by size, "from" prices); `package-builder` (Plus) |
| When can you take it, and how long do you keep it? | `availability` (the next start from the diary sheet); days on every package |
| Ceramic or film? What's the difference? Do I need both? | `explained` (Plus); the coating and film pages; `protection-3d` (Signature) |
| What does film stop? Chips, scratches, dents? Does it heal? | `explained`; the film page |
| Will I see the film? Will it yellow? Does removal damage paint? | film page; questions (the studio's own answers) |
| Which brand of film or coating, and what warranty? | coating and film pages (the studio's own brands and terms, never invented) |
| Do I need correction first? How many stages for mine? | `paint-request` (Signature: photos and answers on WhatsApp); `correction-3d` |
| Will correction thin my clear coat? | correction page; questions (the studio measures first) |
| Can I see work on cars like mine? | `before-after` (Plus: the studio's own pairs, same car only); `gallery` |
| How's my car getting on? When can I collect? | `car-progress` (Signature: by the code on the booking text) |
| When can I wash it? Can I drive in the rain? | `first-wash` (the studio's care steps into the calendar); aftercare page |
| How often should it come back? | `maintenance` (Plus: the plans as calendar reminders) |
| Do you collect, including from the dealer? Where is it kept? | questions; the studio page |
| Do you do interiors, supercars, classics, matte paint? | general pages (`custom-pages`) |
| Is there a deposit? | the book page; the studio's own Stripe link if it uses one |
