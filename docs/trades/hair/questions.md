# Hair salons: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `hair`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Flagship hair salon (hair kit, branch `trade/hair`)
Researched 5–6 Nov 2026 from real UK salons' own pages, described by type: two London colour houses' new-client and
policy pages, a West End salon's visit page and price-by-stylist booking flow, a London salon's FAQ page, a Soho
salon's booking page with its service menu and client reviews, and price lists from salons in London and Scotland
(including training-salon lists). Every price, time and policy on a client site comes from the client.

| Customers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| How much will it cost for **my** hair? Why is it "from"? | Price by length and stylist level (HAIR-1): the salon's own grid, length shown on a real photograph |
| Why does the price change with the stylist? Who is who? | Levels explained in one line each (stylist, senior, director); prices shown after choosing a level or a person, as real booking flows do |
| Which stylist should I book? Who is good with curls / blonde / short cuts? | Stylists with their work and next free day (HAIR-2); stylist match on Signature (HAIR-8) |
| How long will it take? Will balayage take all day? | Times beside every price, by length (HAIR-1) |
| Do I need a skin (patch) test? When? Can I walk in for it? | Colour page and questions: the salon's rule (commonly at least 48 hours before, for new clients or after six months without colour), walk-in hours, home kit if offered |
| Do I need a consultation first? Is it free? | Colour consultation on WhatsApp (HAIR-4): photos now, then the salon's own consultation slot |
| What if my hair is long or thick, or it's been months since colour? | Length grid and the salon's own surcharge line, never invented |
| How often will I need to come back for colour? | Colour upkeep planner (HAIR-5), from the salon's own weeks per service |
| Can I see the colour / cut I want on real people? | Look book by cut, colour and texture (HAIR-3); before/after only with written consent (HAIR-7) |
| Do you cut my texture? (curly, coily, braids) | Curly and textured hair page; stylist filters |
| Is there a deposit? What if I cancel or I'm late? | Policies in plain words (deposit, notice period, lateness, no-show), the salon's own |
| What if I'm not happy? | The salon's re-do window and terms, in their words |
| Can I have colour while pregnant or breastfeeding? | Questions: the salon's policy and a link to NHS guidance; never our advice |
| Wedding hair: trials, when to book, can you come to me? | Wedding hair planner (HAIR-9) and Bridal and occasion page |
| Which products do you use? Can I buy them? | Product shelf (HAIR-6), what the salon really stocks |
| Can I bring someone / my dog? Is there somewhere to wait? | Plan your visit and questions |
| Gift vouchers? | Shared `vouchers` (the client's Stripe link) |
