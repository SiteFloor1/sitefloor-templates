# Tailors: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `tailor`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Bespoke tailor (tailor kit, branch `trade/tailor`)
Researched 2 Nov 2026 from real UK houses' own pages (Dege & Skinner, Kent & Haste and Cad & the Dandy FAQs; Mark
Powell, Gresham Blake and Zebel in the same search). Every number on a client site comes from the client.

| Customers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| What do you make? Bespoke, made to measure or ready to wear? | `garments` (what the house makes, in its words) |
| How much does a suit cost? | `garments` "from" prices, the client's own, or "priced at your first appointment" |
| How long does it take? | `the-process` and the process page, in the house's words (houses quote about 8–12 weeks for a first suit) |
| How many fittings, and what happens at each? | process page (first appointment, cut, baste, forward, finished) |
| Who will I see? | the house and its cutters (`team`); "the same cutter at every fitting" in the questions |
| Which cloths, how many, which weight for which season? | `cloth-book` and the cloth library by season, each weave full screen |
| Can I see a swatch before I come? | "Ask for a swatch" on each cloth (WhatsApp) |
| Where is my suit now? When is my next fitting? | `commission` by code (Signature), next fitting to the calendar |
| Do you come to my city or abroad? | `travels`: trunk shows by city with dates and a calendar file |
| I'm getting married: when must I start? | `wedding-order` from one date (Signature) |
| What do lapel, vent, ticket pocket mean? | `lexicon` (Signature) |
| Do you make for women? | the questions, in the house's words |
| Can it be altered later, and do you alter other makers' suits? | the questions (inlays; alterations policy) |
| Do you keep my pattern? | the process page (the pattern is kept in the client's name) |
| Do I need an appointment, when are you open, where exactly, parking? | Plan your visit (address, station, parking, access, hours) |
| Deposit, payment, returns? | the questions and Plan your visit ("payment"), in the house's words; bespoke is usually not returnable except for faults |
