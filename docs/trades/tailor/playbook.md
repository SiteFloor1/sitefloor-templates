# Tailors: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `tailor`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Bespoke tailor (new `tailor` kit), built 4 Nov 2026
- Customer: someone commissioning a first bespoke suit or coat (often for a wedding or a new role), or a returning
  client between fittings. The site's job: get the first-appointment request with the garments in mind, and keep
  returning clients informed without a phone call.
- Demo brand **Fennick & Lowe** (fictional; searched, no tailor uses it), a yard off Savile Row, by appointment. Owner
  chose direction A, Flannel, then the cutting-room revision (4 Nov). Look `flannel`, demo `sites/demos/tailor`, design
  notes `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/tailor.md`.
- **Starter:** `garments` (what the house makes with "from" prices; request sheet on WhatsApp), `the-process` (the
  steps in a line each; the easy extra).
- **Plus:** `cloth-book` (the book opened, weave full screen, "Ask for a swatch", and the easy extra: a set of up to
  five cloths asked for in one WhatsApp message, kept on the visitor's phone), `cloth-page` (by season),
  `process-page`, `travels` (trunk shows by city, .ics, request a slot), `garment-pages` (what we make + a page per
  garment; a collection, counted as one page), `explained` (bespoke vs made to measure vs ready to wear, sourced and
  dated), `journal` (dated articles, each a page; one page), `voucher-page` (with shared `vouchers`).
- **Signature (showpieces):** `commission` (by code from a published sheet: code, stage, next fitting, cloth, updated;
  nothing personal), `spec-card` (lapel, fastening, pockets and cloth from the house's own photographs → one card, sent
  on WhatsApp with a link that reopens it, or printed), `wedding-order` (the wedding party planner: date + party size
  → start date by the house's own rule, every fitting in one .ics, one cloth for everyone), `lexicon`, `reorder` (from
  your pattern: order code, garment, cloth, any change, on WhatsApp; no measurement asked or stored). The spec card's
  groups can be for some garments only (`for`), so a shirt gets its collar. `fittingReminder` (optional) puts an alarm
  the evening before, with what to bring, in the commission and wedding calendar files. Plus shared
  `custom-pages` for the house's story and alterations and care.
- **Alternatives (not built):** your pattern on file (re-order "the same trousers in a new cloth"); cloth in three
  lights (daylight, tungsten, evening photos per cloth); the coat opened in 3D (canvas, chest piece, padded lapel; only
  at flagship quality, photo first, loads on tap); an occasion order-by for the Season; a gift-a-commission voucher by the client's Stripe link;
  a cloth reserved for you (a length held for a week, asked for on WhatsApp); a fitting-room chooser for trunk shows
  (pick the slot on the day's own times).
- Spec card gaps: vents (centre, side, none) and patch pockets need real photographs; the groups are config, so a
  house adds them with its own photos.
- Honesty: requests, never "booked"; prices, weights, weeks and fittings come from the client; no mill names or logos
  unless the house sells that cloth and says so; the commission sheet holds no names or measurements; general facts on
  the bespoke page link to their sources with the month checked; the party rule (extra weeks by size) is the house's own.
