# Holiday-let and Airbnb management: customer questions

What property owners and guests ask a holiday-let (short-let, Airbnb) management company in the UK before they get in
touch. Drawn from the owner FAQ, "let with us", fees and onboarding pages of UK management companies and holiday-cottage
agencies, Airbnb's own host and co-host pages, and the themes that recur in their reviews (being kept informed, surprise
deductions, cleaning standards, damage, slow payouts, getting the home back for their own use). Rules are from GOV.UK and
legislation.gov.uk, checked 26 Nov 2026. The kit covers England; Scotland (licensing) and Wales have their own rules and
the site links to them rather than explaining them.

The owners are the management company's real customers, so their questions come first and shape the home page.

## Owners

| # | Question | What the site must answer |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | What could my home earn with you? | An estimate worked out only from the company's own published figures for homes like it (nightly rate and nights booked last year, by size and area), with the basis, the date and "not a promise" on the same screen. Never market data we invent |
| O2 | What does it cost? What's your fee, in pounds? Is VAT in it? Any set-up fee, minimum term, exit fee? | The fee as a percentage and as pounds on the estimate, VAT included in the headline, every other charge (set-up, photography, linen, consumables, exit) listed once on the fees page |
| O3 | What exactly do you do, and what's left for me? | A plain list: listing and photography, pricing, guest messages, check-in, cleaning and linen, maintenance, payments; and what stays the owner's (insurance, safety certificates, council tax or business rates, tax returns) |
| O4 | What happens between guests? Who cleans, how do I know it's done properly? | The turnover step by step, with times: check-out, linen, clean, inspection, restock, check-in; how the owner sees it (inspection photos, a note) |
| O5 | Which platforms will my home be on? Do I keep my own Airbnb account? | Where homes are listed (Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, the company's own site) and whether the company co-hosts on the owner's account or lists under its own |
| O6 | When and how do I get paid? What will my statement show? | Payout timing and a sample monthly statement: nights, what guests paid, the fee in pounds, costs, what reached the owner. Sample clearly marked |
| O7 | Can I still use the home myself? | How owner stays are blocked out, how much notice, and how that affects the estimate |
| O8 | Who are the guests? What if something is damaged or a party happens? | Guest screening, house rules, the deposit or damage cover the company uses (as the client states it), who handles a claim |
| O9 | What do I need before I can let it? Is it legal here? | Safety basics (fire, gas, electrics, carbon monoxide, EPC), insurance, planning, business rates, and the local rules (London's 90-night limit, Scotland's licence, England's coming register), each linked to the official page with the date checked |
| O10 | Do you manage homes like mine near me? | The homes the company looks after, by area, with real photographs; the areas it covers (postcode districts) |
| O11 | How do we start, and how long until the first guest? | Onboarding steps with honest timings in the company's own words: visit, contract, set-up (photos, linen, lockbox), first listing |
| O12 | What do other owners say? | Real owner reviews copied with name and date, or nothing |
| O13 | Can I leave? What's the notice? | Contract term and notice period, and what happens to future bookings if they leave |

## Guests

| # | Question | What the site must answer |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Which homes do you have, and is it free on my dates? | The homes with photographs, sleeps, bedrooms and a link to book on the platform or the client's booking page; availability only from a live source, never shown when stale |
| G2 | What's the total price? Any cleaning or service fee? | Prices shown as the total for the stay including every mandatory fee (CMA rules), never a nightly rate with fees added later |
| G3 | How do I get in? What time is check-in and check-out? | Check-in and check-out times, self check-in or meet-and-greet, the code arriving before the day |
| G4 | Is there parking, Wi-Fi, a cot, a dog bed? Is it accessible? | The home's facts and an access statement |
| G5 | What's nearby: beach, pub, shop, station? | A guest guidebook for each home (walks, food, the nearest shop and pharmacy) |
| G6 | Who do I call if something goes wrong? | The guest line and hours, what counts as an emergency |
| G7 | Can I bring my dog? Can we arrive early or leave late? | Per-home rules and how to ask |
| G8 | What's the cancellation policy and the deposit? | The terms, as the client states them, linked from every price |
