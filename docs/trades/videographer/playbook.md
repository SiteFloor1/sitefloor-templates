# Videographers and photographers: playbook

What to build for this trade (no kit yet). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## The plan (from the original trade list)

9. VIDEOGRAPHERS AND PHOTOGRAPHERS. Demo: fictional
   Customers ask: Can I see work like mine? What's in a package? Are you free on my date?
   How long until I get it? Drone?
   Core: a showreel and Our work filtered by type (wedding, music video, brand); packages;
   questions.
   Plus: one page per type; a "how a shoot works" timeline.
   SIGNATURE, "Build your shoot":
   - pick the type, hours, extras (drone, second shooter, highlights film, raw files) and
     delivery speed;
   - live price;
   - a date checker against booked dates from a Sheet;
   - "Hold this date" on WhatsApp; deposit by Payment Link.
   Extras: a cinematic hero with a chaptered showreel (lightweight: poster image and
   lazy-loaded video).
