# Financial advisers: customer questions

What people ask an independent or restricted financial adviser before a first meeting, from UK advice firms' public
pages (Schroders Personal Wealth, Coutts, unbiased.co.uk firm profiles, Aviva and M&G advice pages) and the FCA's own
consumer pages. Checked 16 Nov 2026. Where the kit answers each (codes: proposed list in `playbook.md`):

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| What will advice cost me, in pounds: the first piece of advice and each year after? | The fee slip in the hero (IFA-8) and the Fees page (IFA-2); Starter shows three worked examples (IFA-5) |
| Do you charge a percentage, a fixed fee or by the hour? Is there a minimum fee? | Fees page: the firm's own published structure, with minimums and the hourly rate |
| Are fund and platform charges on top? | Fees page and the slip's footnote: yes, separate, and the firm shows them before you agree |
| Is there a minimum amount I need to invest? | Services (IFA-1, `services.minimum`, the firm's own figure); said plainly when there is none |
| Are you independent or restricted, and what is the difference? | Independent or restricted explained (IFA-6), in the firm's own words of its status |
| Are you authorised by the FCA? What is your number, and can I check it? | Footer on every page (FRN with a link to the FCA Register); each adviser's individual reference number (IFA-1) |
| What do you help with: pensions, retirement income, inheritance tax, protection, mortgages? | What we help with (home register) and a page per area |
| When can I take my pension? When do I get my State Pension? | Retirement milestones (IFA-9): dates from GOV.UK's tables, a link to GOV.UK's own checker, no growth figures |
| Is the first meeting free? How long is it, and is it in person or on video? | Your first meeting (IFA-3, IFA-7) |
| What should I bring? | The first-meeting checklist (IFA-3), per topic (pension statements, State Pension forecast, will, policies) |
| How often will we review things? | Fees page: what the ongoing fee buys (review each year, as the firm states) |
| Who will I deal with? | Advisers (IFA-1): photo, what each looks after, register link |
| How do I send you documents safely? | Client documents (IFA-10): a link to the firm's own client portal, with a fraud warning first |
| How do I know an email or call is really from you? | Is this really us? (IFA-11) |
| How do I complain? | Regulatory information: the firm first, then the Financial Ombudsman Service (free; 0800 023 4567) |

## Rules checked (16 Nov 2026)

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Every promotion fair, clear and not misleading (COBS 4.2.1R); make clear when capital is at risk (COBS 4.2.4G) | https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/COBS/4/2.html |
| Balanced benefits and risks; "capital at risk" alone is often ineffective, so the kit uses a plain sentence | https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/risk-warnings-mainstream-investments |
| Adviser charges disclosed in cash terms, initial and ongoing, before the client pays | https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/adviser-charging-rules and COBS 6.1A |
| Firms describe their advice as independent or restricted (COBS 6.2B) | https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/COBS/6/2B.html |
| Complaints: the firm, then the Financial Ombudsman Service | https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/complaints and https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/contact-us |
| Normal minimum pension age rises from 55 to 57 on 6 April 2028 | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/increasing-normal-minimum-pension-age |
| State Pension age rising from 66 to 67 between 2026 and 2028; the checker | https://www.gov.uk/state-pension-age |

Never on an adviser site: projected or past returns, "guaranteed", growth illustrations, ratings in structured data,
or an adviser's opinion presented as advice. Articles are general information, ticked by the firm's compliance.
