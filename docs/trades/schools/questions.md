## Small independent prep and senior schools (design round 1, branch `trade/schools`)
Written 28 Nov 2026 from what parents ask on real UK independent schools' admissions, fees, FAQ and open-event pages
and from gov.uk. The customer is the parent (often both parents, sometimes a grandparent paying fees, sometimes a
family abroad or a forces family). The child is 3 to 18. Every fee, date, deadline, house, bus route, policy and
result on a client site comes from the school.

Rules that hold on every client site:
- **Safeguarding:** no identifiable child's face anywhere (photographs, film, stock or the school's own). Buildings,
  grounds, rooms, hands, objects, backs and distance only. Never a child's name with a picture.
- **Fees:** exactly as the school publishes them, labelled per term or per year, **including VAT** (see below), with the
  date the school's figures were checked. Never invent or summarise bursary or scholarship terms; link the school's
  own policy.
- **Inspection (SCH-8):** link the real report (ISI for schools in an ISC association, Ofsted for others). Never quote
  selectively, never paraphrase an outcome, never put it in a badge.
- **Results:** only as the school publishes them, with the year. Never a league-table or ranking claim.

VAT checked on gov.uk, 28 Nov 2026:
- Since 1 January 2025, education and vocational training provided by a private school for a charge, and boarding
  closely related to it, are standard-rated at 20%
  ([VAT Notice 701/30](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-notice-70130-education-and-vocational-training)).
- Fees paid from 29 July 2024 for terms starting on or after 1 January 2025 are caught too
  ([policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-on-private-school-fees)).
- Nursery classes made up wholly or almost wholly (about 90% or more) of children under compulsory school age stay
  exempt ([register guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-must-register-for-vat-if-you-receive-private-school-fees)).
  So a school with a nursery shows nursery fees as the school states them, never with VAT added by us.
- Some goods and services the school supplies may be treated differently
  ([charging and reclaiming guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charging-and-reclaiming-vat-on-goods-and-services-related-to-private-school-fees)):
  the kit shows each extra exactly as the school words it and never works VAT out itself.

| Parents ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Which year group would my child join, and when? | SCH-5 admissions timeline (Plus): date of birth in, the year group and September for every entry point the school offers, 1 September cut-off |
| When do I need to register? When is the assessment? Am I too late? | SCH-5: the school's own deadlines worked out for that child, past ones marked, one calendar file |
| What are the fees? Per term or per year? Does that include VAT? | SCH-2 fees as published (Starter): the school's table, VAT included and stated, date checked |
| What's extra? Lunch, trips, music lessons, uniform, the bus, exam fees? | SCH-2: the school's own list of extras in its words; "included" only where the school says so |
| Is there a sibling discount, a forces discount, a bursary? | Fees page: the school's wording and a link to its bursary policy; we never write terms |
| Do you offer scholarships? For what, and when are they assessed? | Admissions page: the school's awards and dates; SCH-5 can carry scholarship deadlines |
| When are the open days? Can we have a private tour? | SCH-1 open days and visit request (Starter): the next event, a request by email or WhatsApp |
| Is there boarding? Full, weekly, flexi? From what age? What does it cost? | SCH-7 boarding houses (Signature), the Boarding page, fees as published |
| How do I get there? Is there a school bus from our town? | Plan your visit (every plan); school bus routes and pick-up times (proposed, see the playbook) |
| Can I read the inspection report? | SCH-8 inspection reports linked (Signature, proposed for Plus): the real report, never paraphrased |
| What does a day look like? When does it start and finish? Is there wraparound care? | SCH-4 a day in the life (Plus): the school's own timings |
| What are the term dates? Exeats, half terms, INSET days? | SCH-3 term dates as a calendar file (Plus) |
| How big are the classes? Is it selective? | Admissions and Academic pages, in the school's words only |
| What are the exam results? | News and journal / Academic: results only as the school publishes them, with the year |
| How do you look after children? Who do I talk to if I'm worried? | Pastoral care page and the school's safeguarding policy, linked; the DSL's contact as the school gives it |
| What about SEND, EAL, learning support? | Pastoral or Academic page, in the school's words |
| What sport, music, drama is there? | Sport and Arts pages |
| What is lunch like? Allergies? | Life at the school; the school's allergy statement |
| Where do children go afterwards (senior school, university)? | Academic page, as the school publishes it |
| Is it a Church of England / Catholic / non-denominational school? | Home and About, in the school's words |
| Uniform: where do we buy it? Second-hand? | Life at the school, linking the school's supplier |
| We live abroad / are a forces family. Can you help? | Admissions page, the school's own wording (e.g. CEA for forces families, guardianship) |
| How do I apply? What do I send? | Admissions page: the school's steps, its registration form or link, the fee as published |
