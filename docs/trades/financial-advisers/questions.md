# Financial advisers: customer questions

What people in the UK ask an independent or restricted financial adviser before they book or sign, ranked by how often
the answer decides whether they go ahead. Sources (checked 16 Nov 2026): MoneyHelper's guide to adviser fees and its
questions to ask an adviser; Which?'s guide to the cost of advice; Unbiased's first-meeting guide and adviser listings
(how UK firms present a free first meeting, minimums and independent or restricted); public fee pages of UK advice
firms, including Lloyds Wealth (formerly Schroders Personal Wealth: 1.75% initial, 0.65% ongoing up to £1m);
the FCA Handbook (COBS 4.2, COBS 6.2B, GEN 4) and the FCA's page on risk warnings for mainstream investments
(11 Dec 2025); GOV.UK on State Pension age and the normal minimum pension age.

Where the proposed kit answers each (codes in [`playbook.md`](playbook.md)):

| # | Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What will advice cost me, in pounds, to start and every year? | IFA-2 fees page; IFA-6 Fees in pounds |
| 2 | Is the first meeting free, and what happens in it? | IFA-3 first meeting; home |
| 3 | Are you independent or restricted, and what does that mean for me? | IFA-7 independent or restricted (always on) |
| 4 | Are you FCA authorised? What's your register number, and can I check it? | IFA-1 FCA line on every page, register link; IFA-5 check it's us |
| 5 | Is there a minimum amount you'll work with? | IFA-1 who we help (the firm's own figure) |
| 6 | How are you paid? Do you take commission? | IFA-2 fees page (adviser charging; commission only where the law allows, e.g. protection) |
| 7 | What exactly do I get for the ongoing fee? How often do we review? | IFA-2 fees page, what's included; IFA-9 your year with us |
| 8 | Can I pay for one-off advice instead of ongoing? | IFA-2 fees page (fixed or one-off fees if offered) |
| 9 | What other charges will I pay (platform, funds)? | IFA-2 and IFA-6: separate, shown in pounds in writing before you decide |
| 10 | Who will I actually deal with, and what are their qualifications? | IFA-1 advisers (Chartered, DipPFS etc. as the firm states), each with their FCA individual reference |
| 11 | Can you help me retire, and when can I take my pension? | Pensions and retirement page; IFA-10 Milestones |
| 12 | When is my State Pension age? | IFA-10 Milestones (links to GOV.UK's checker for the exact date) |
| 13 | Should I combine my old pensions? | Pensions page (general information, then a meeting) |
| 14 | Can you help reduce inheritance tax? | Inheritance tax page (general information, no promises) |
| 15 | Do you advise on protection (life cover, income protection)? | Protection page |
| 16 | Do you arrange mortgages? | Mortgages page, with its own regulatory wording |
| 17 | What should I bring to the first meeting? | IFA-3 first-meeting checklist |

| 22 | Is my money protected if the firm goes bust? | Regulatory information (FSCS, only as it applies to the firm's business) |
| 23 | Do you only work with wealthy people? | Who we help; the minimum stated plainly |

| 27 | How do I send you documents safely? | IFA-8 secure upload link (the firm's own portal) |

| 29 | Do you offer ethical or sustainable investment advice? | Approach page (only if the firm offers it) |
| 30 | Can you help with long-term care costs for a parent? | Later life page (if offered; specialist accreditation named only if held) |
| 31 | What happens to my plan if my adviser leaves? | Your year with us |
| 32 | Do you charge VAT? | Fees page (the firm states which fees carry VAT) |

Never on any site: projected returns, "guaranteed", "best", performance figures or calculators that project growth.
