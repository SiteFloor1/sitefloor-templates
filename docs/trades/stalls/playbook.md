# Street-food and dessert stalls: playbook

Who they are: stalls that sell one thing well (bubble waffles, Asian pancakes, churros, candy floss, bubble tea, loaded
fries) at markets, night markets and pop-ups, and take private parties. Their customers find them on Instagram and
TikTok; the site's job is the question those posts can't answer: **where are you this week, and what's on the menu?**

## Kit: `stalls`

- **Starter:** where to find us this week (`stalls-where`), the menu with prices and allergens (`stalls-menu`).
- **Plus:** latest posts (`stalls-posts`: Behold for Instagram, TikTok as links, never embeds), book us for your party
  (`stalls-book`: date check, then a WhatsApp enquiry), pitch finder (`stalls-pitch`, client-only), menu, find-us,
  parties and allergens pages.
- **Signature:** next-stop countdown (`stalls-next`), sold out today (`stalls-sold-out`), the board saved as a picture
  (`stalls-board`), set-up card (`stalls-setup`), drop alert (`stalls-drop`).
- **Add-on:** rain check (`stalls-rain`, MET Norway forecast for the next stop, through the shared weather function).

## Rules for this trade

- The stall's week lives in a Google Sheet (date, place, open, close, status, note); a stale sheet shows the usual week,
  never a guess. Cancelled stops say so.
- Party dates are "open", "few left" or "taken"; the site never says "booked" or takes payment.
- Allergens use the 14 UK allergens; the hygiene rating links to ratings.food.gov.uk only. Demo charts and ratings are
  labelled samples.
- Demos are fictional: no links to real Instagram or TikTok accounts. Clients add their own profiles.
- The pitch finder needs the client's own photo of their pitch, so it is client-only.
