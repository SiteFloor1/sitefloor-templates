# Wedding and event stalls: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch. How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

Mobile bars (horsebox, prosecco van, cocktail bar), gelato and ice-cream carts, candy floss and dessert carts hired for
weddings, corporate events and private parties. Gathered 17 Nov 2026 from UK supplier listings on Bridebook, Add to Event,
Poptop and Rock My Wedding (horsebox bars in Kent, Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire, Liverpool, York and the South East). A
first pass: no figures are quoted here; a client's site always uses the client's own prices, rules and paperwork.

## Price and packages (asked first)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| How much for my number of guests? | packages by guests (STALL-1); menu and packages page |
| What is included: staff, glassware, cups and cones, napkins, set-up? | packages; quote builder (STALL-3) |
| Is it a fixed fee, a cash bar, or a mix (a tab up to an amount, then guests pay)? | quote builder; menu and packages |
| Dry hire or fully stocked and staffed? | packages; quote builder |
| Is travel included, and how far do you come? | quote builder (travel by postcode); venues served (STALL-6) |
| How long is service, and what does an extra hour cost? | quote builder |
| Deposit, when the balance is due, and what if we cancel or move the date? | quote; terms (client's own) |

## The venue (the questions venues ask too)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| How much space do you need, and will it fit through the gate or doors? | set-up requirements (STALL-5) |

## Trust and rules (client-supplied facts only)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Are you insured (public liability)? Can the venue have the certificate? | set-up card (paperwork line, client's own document) |
| What is your food hygiene rating? | client-supplied rating with a link to the official Food Standards Agency listing |
| Allergen information for each flavour or drink | client-supplied allergen sheet, linked |
| Who holds the personal licence? Do we need a Temporary Event Notice, or does the venue's licence cover it? | client-supplied facts, linked to the gov.uk pages on personal licences and Temporary Event Notices; never our own legal advice |
| Do you serve under-18s alcohol-free, and how do you check ID? | client-supplied policy |
| Can we see real weddings you've done, and reviews? | gallery; reviews (copied from Google with name and date) |
