# Street-food and dessert stalls: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch. How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

Instagram- and TikTok-led food stalls (Asian pancakes, candy floss, churros, bubble waffles, bubble tea, loaded fries) that
trade at markets and pop-ups and take some private parties. Rarely wedding suppliers. Gathered 17 Nov 2026 from the owner's
brief and the comments and captions such stalls get (where are you today, are you sold out, do you do parties). A first
pass: no figures are quoted; a client's site always uses the client's own prices, places and rules.

## Where and when (asked most)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Where are you today? Where this week? | where to find us this week (Starter, from the stall's sheet) |
| What time do you open, and when do you pack up? | where to find us; next stop countdown |
| How do I get there? Which end of the market? | Directions link per stop (maps search link, no paid map) |
| Are you still there, or sold out? | "sold out today" from the sheet (Signature) |
| When are you next near me? | the week's stops; Instagram for one-offs |

## The food
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What's on the menu and how much? | menu with prices (Starter) |
| What's in it: gluten, egg, milk, nuts, soya? Is anything vegan? | allergens beside each item (Starter, client-supplied) |
| What's new or only on this weekend? | the board; the latest posts (Plus) |
| How big is it, is it enough to share? | menu notes; photos |
| Do you take card? | the stop card (client says) |

## Parties and private hire (lower on the page)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Can you come to my party, office or school fair? Is my date free? | book us for your party: date check and a WhatsApp enquiry (Plus) |
| How many can you serve, and what does it cost? | party enquiry (client quotes on WhatsApp) |
| What do you need: space, power, a gazebo, water? | set-up card (Signature, forwardable) |
| Do you bring your own gazebo and lighting? | set-up card |

## Trust and rules (client-supplied facts only)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What's your food hygiene rating? | the client's rating with a link to its Food Standards Agency listing |
| Are you insured? (organisers and venues ask) | set-up card, the client's own certificate on request |
| Is it fresh, made in front of me? | photos and film of the stall; never claims the client hasn't made |
| Can I see what it really looks like? | the latest posts (Behold for Instagram; TikTok as links) |
