# Nail, lash and beauty salons: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `nails`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Nail and beauty
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Can I see your work for the style I want? | `work` filtered by hashtag (#chrome, #french, #almond…) |
| Who's best for nail art / acrylics / BIAB / natural nails? | team filters |
| How long will my set last, and how long does it take? | `price-page` times; questions |
| Do you do removals or fixes from another salon? | `price-list`; questions |
| Are your products vegan / cruelty-free? Allergies? | questions |
| Do I pay a deposit? What if I cancel? | questions (policies) |
| Is there a free slot this week? | booking app link; free-slot grid (Atelier) |

## Nail salon (nails kit, look `quail`)
From real UK salons' FAQs and booking pages (Shoreditch Nails, Townhouse) and customers' own threads; the full list is in
`sitefloor-internal/docs/design/nail-salon.md`.

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What will this colour look like on me? | `shade-hand` (the salon's photos of one hand in several shades); `shade-on-hand` on Signature (any shade, three skin tones) |
| What shades do you have? Do you have this season's colour? | `shades-page` (every shade, number, system, finish, by family); `monthly-edit` on the home page |
| Gel, BIAB or acrylic: which should I have? Is BIAB bad for my nails? | `gel-biab` side by side; questions |
| How long does it take and what does it cost? | `menu-hours` (by 30, 60, 90 minutes, with prices); `build-set` adds up a whole set (Signature) |
| How is nail art priced? Can I add it on the day? | `art-page` (sets by level, shape and length, each with time and price) |
| When's the next free slot? | `next-slot` (the salon's own Sheet; old data says "message us") |
| Is removal included? Will you remove another salon's gel? | `nail-menu` policies; questions |
| What if it chips? How often do I need an infill? | policies; `infill-reminder` (the infill day in the calendar) |
| When should I book a bridal trial and the final set? | `bridal-page`; `bridal-timeline` works the dates back from the wedding (Signature) |
| Can we come as a hen or bridal party? | `party-booking` (Signature) |
| Can I book my usual again quickly? | `usual-set` (remembered on the visitor's own phone) |
| Where are you, step-free, parking, toilet? Can I bring my baby or a friend? | Plan your visit; questions |
