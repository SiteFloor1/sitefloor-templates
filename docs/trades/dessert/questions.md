# Dessert shops and chocolatiers: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `bakery`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Dessert shop (bakery kit, trade/dessert)
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Are you open late tonight? Are you open now? | late opening hours with "open now" (hero and `visit`) |
| Is it busy, can we sit in? | `questions` (seating, dine-in or takeaway); parties page for big groups |
| What's on tonight? Is the kunafa or cheesecake sold out? | "Tonight's counter" from the shop's Sheet (available, low, sold out; old rows never shown) |
| Eggless, vegan, halal (gelatine), nut-free? | dessert menu: the FSA's 14 allergens per item, eggless / vegan / halal marks from the shop, "hide items containing…"; "Please tell us about any allergy when you order" |
| Can I order a celebration cake? How far ahead, how much, can it have a name on it? | celebration cakes page (sizes, flavours, lead time, prices from the shop); Cake Builder on Signature (price, earliest date, message) |
| Can I collect at a set time? | collection-slot request on WhatsApp |
| Do you deliver (Deliveroo, Uber Eats)? | delivery app links |
| Can you do parties and big groups? | parties and groups page |
| Can I pay a deposit or pay online? | `pay-link` (the shop's own Stripe Payment Link) |
| Gift cards? | gift `vouchers` |
| Prices? | dessert menu and cake price table (the shop's own prices, never guessed) |
| What does it look like, what do people order? | `gallery` from Instagram |
| Can you post a box? When will it arrive? | `post-box`: posting days, cut-off, "usually with them Friday" (skips bank holidays and closures) |
| Can I take gelato home? | `gelato-tubs` (Plus) |
| Do you do favours for a wedding or company gifts? | `favours` by quantity (Plus); "your seal" with their initials (Signature) |
| How long do chocolates keep, and how do I store them? | `questions` (the shop's own answer) |
| Do you do an advent calendar? | `advent-box` (Signature, seasonal) |
