# Car videographers and photographers: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (no kit yet). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

Two kinds of customer: **private owners** (a new car, a classic, a modified build, a car being sold) and **dealers and brands**
(stock and hero films for listings, launches and social). Codes are the approved features in `docs/VISION.md` (CVID-1 to CVID-12; CVID-6 dropped).
Every price, timing, permit, licence, insurance and policy shown on a client site is the client's own.

## Private owners
| Owners ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Can I see work on a car like mine (make, era, colour, modified)? | CVID-3 portfolio by brand; the showreel (CVID-1) |
| What does a shoot cost, everything included? | CVID-1 packages: one all-in price per package (travel, edit and any mandatory fee inside the headline price) |
| What do I actually get: how many photos, how long is the film? | CVID-7 delivery formats; package cards |
| Will it look right on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and a sale listing? | CVID-7 delivery formats (every frame sized for its platform) |
| What time will we shoot on my date, and how long does it take? | CVID-8 tonight's light; CVID-5 golden-hour planner (real sun times for the place and date); package length |
| Where do we shoot? Can you come to my house? | CVID-4 location guide; questions |
| How do rolling shots work? Is it safe, and who drives? | Rolling shots page (8); Questions page (13): the client's own method and rules) |
| What if it rains? | CVID-11 shoot-day cloud forecast (labelled as a forecast); Questions page (13): the client's own weather and rebooking policy |
| Do I need to get the car cleaned first? | questions; a link to a detailer the client works with, if any |
| Can you hide my number plate? | questions; delivery notes |
| Do you fly a drone? | Questions page (13): the client's own Flyer ID and Operator ID, never assumed) |
| Can I post the film with music? | questions (licensed tracks; the client's own wording) |
| How soon do I get it? | CVID-9 turnaround dates; package cards |
| Are you free on my date, and how do I book? | Book a shoot (5); WhatsApp message with the date, car and package; CVID-12 share card for the plan |
| What do you need from me on the day? | CVID-2 shot list builder; "Behind the shoot" (12) |

## Dealers and brands
| Dealers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Can you shoot our stock consistently, week after week? | Dealerships and brands page (6); packages |
| How fast do we get listing photos? | CVID-9 turnaround dates; CVID-10 listing shot set |
| What sizes do we get for the website, the portal listing and social? | CVID-7 delivery formats; Deliveries page (14) |
| Can we use the photos and films in paid ads? | Questions page (13): the client's own usage terms) |
| Are you insured to shoot on our forecourt or at a launch? | Questions page (13): the client's own cover, never assumed) |
| Can we plan the shots before the day? | CVID-2 shot list builder (sent as one WhatsApp or email summary) |
