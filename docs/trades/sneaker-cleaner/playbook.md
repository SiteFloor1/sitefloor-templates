## The plan (from the original trade list)

10. SNEAKER AND CREP CLEANERS (the niche found earlier). Demo: fictional
    Customers ask: Can you save my shoes? Suede? Price? How long? Postal service?
    Core: before/after slider (shared); prices by service; Our work by brand.
    Plus: postal service page with step-by-step how to send; aftercare.
    SIGNATURE, "Clean builder":
    - pick shoe type and material (canvas, leather, suede, mesh) and the services
      (deep clean, sole whitening, re-dye, ice soles, protection);
    - a drawn shoe updates;
    - price and turnaround;
    - "Book drop-off" or "Send by post" with the order on WhatsApp.
    Extras: an order-status lookup from a Sheet by order number (fails safe).
