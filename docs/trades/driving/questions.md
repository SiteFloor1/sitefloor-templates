# Driving instructors: customer questions

What learners (and the parents paying) ask before they book, most asked first. Every answer comes from the
instructor or from gov.uk/DVSA; the site links to gov.uk for anything that is a rule.

## Prices
- How much is a lesson? Is a two-hour lesson cheaper per hour?
- Do you do block bookings, and is there a discount?
- How much is an intensive course, all in? Does it include the test fee?
- Is automatic more expensive than manual?
- Do I pay per lesson or up front? Can I pay by card or bank transfer?
- What happens if I cancel? How much notice do you need?
- Do you charge for the car on test day?
- What do the DVSA fees cost (provisional licence, theory test, practical test)? → link to gov.uk, never quoted by the site.

## Availability
- When is your next free lesson? Do you have evenings or weekends?
- Can you start this week?
- How soon could I take an intensive course?
- Can you pick me up from home, school, college or work? Do you cover my postcode?
- Can you take me to my test if I've already booked it?

## "Do you do…"
- Automatic, manual, or both?
- Intensive or semi-intensive courses?
- Pass Plus? Motorway lessons?
- Refresher lessons for people who passed years ago or are nervous?
- Lessons for people who've failed with another instructor?
- Lessons in my own car?
- Female instructor? Instructors who speak another language?
- Help with the theory test and hazard perception?
- Mock tests on the real test routes?

## Trust
- Are you a fully qualified ADI (approved driving instructor) or a trainee? What's your badge number? → client-supplied field only.
- How long have you been teaching?
- What's your pass rate? → only if the client supplies it, with the dates it covers.
- What car will I learn in? Dual controls?
- Can I see reviews from real pupils?
- Will I have the same instructor every lesson?
- How do I know how I'm getting on? Will you tell me honestly when I'm not ready?

## Rules (always answered with a gov.uk or DVSA link)
- How old do I have to be? What do I need before my first lesson? → provisional licence: gov.uk/apply-first-provisional-driving-licence
- Do I need to pass my theory test first? → gov.uk/book-theory-test
- How do I book, change or cancel my practical test? → gov.uk/book-driving-test, gov.uk/change-driving-test
- What happens on the test? → gov.uk/driving-test/what-happens-during-test
- How many lessons will I need? → the DVSA's Ready to Pass? guidance (gov.uk/ready-to-pass): on average 45 hours of lessons plus 22 hours of private practice.
- Am I ready for my test? → the DVSA's five-point Ready to Pass? checklist.
- Can I practise with a parent? What insurance do I need? → gov.uk/driving-lessons-learning-to-drive/practising-with-family-or-friends
- Where are the test centres near me? → gov.uk/find-driving-test-centre
