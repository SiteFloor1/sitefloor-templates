# Bakers and cake makers: playbook

What to build for this trade (no kit yet). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## The plan (from the original trade list)

4. BAKERIES AND CAKE MAKERS. Demo: fictional
   Customers ask: What's in today? Is it sold out? Custom cake, how far ahead? Allergens,
   eggless, vegan, halal? Collection or delivery?
   Core: "Today's bakes" from a Sheet with live sold-out flags and a fresh-out time;
   allergens; questions.
   Plus: custom cakes, wedding cakes and corporate pages; collection-slot request.
   SIGNATURE, "Cake Builder":
   - choose tiers, size (and how many servings), sponge, filling and finish;
   - a drawn cake updates live;
   - price and the earliest date it can be ready (from lead time plus the Sheet's
     blocked dates);
   - "Request this cake" on WhatsApp with the spec; deposit by Payment Link.
   Extras: a "pre-order for Saturday" list; a seasonal specials board.
   BUILT (Oct 2026, kit `bakery`, look `cacao`, demo Velvet Spoon, a chocolatier and gelateria): also dessert shops,
   pâtisseries and glaciers. Real photography and film only. Starter: the gelato wall and today's counter from the
   Sheet ("only N left today", "back tomorrow"). Plus: the weekly flavour vote (Google Form) as the easy extra,
   Payment Links from Plus. Signature: the box (each piece placed, ribbon tied, card written, sent as a gift with its
   own link) and the tasting flight, then your seal (favour boxes with the customer's initials on the shop's seal)
   and the advent box. Plus also has take-home tubs, posted boxes, a box a month and favours; Starter the kitchen line. Alternative showpiece kept in the box README: "your words on the bar".
   Ideas not built yet: a pre-order calendar for the weekend's pieces; a "the queue right now" line from the Sheet;
   a gift box sent to someone else's door with a card; a pastry advent calendar that opens one window a day.
