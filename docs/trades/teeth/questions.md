# Teeth whitening and aesthetic dentistry: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `smile`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Private whitening and aesthetic dental clinic (smile kit)
Gathered 5–6 Nov 2026 from the kinds of sources patients actually read: UK private clinics' whitening and cosmetic FAQ
pages (London, Manchester and regional practices), treatment-comparison pages (home trays vs in-clinic vs shop kits),
online booking pages (what they ask for before a consultation), Google and Doctify-style reviews (what patients praise
or regret), and patient-advice articles on checking a dentist's registration. Clinical answers are only ever the
clinician's own words from the clinic's `site.json`; the site never promises a shade, a result or a duration.

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Who will actually treat me? Are they a dentist, and are they registered? | clinicians page: each clinician's role, qualification and country, GDC number linked to the GDC register (every plan); the GDC line on every treatment |
| Is whitening at a salon or kiosk legal? Why is a dentist dearer? | whitening page: the clinic's plain statement that only GDC-registered professionals may whiten teeth, linked to the GDC's own page with the date checked |
| How much is it? What's included (consultation, trays, top-up gel)? | treatments and prices (Starter): "from" prices the clinic sets, what's included, deposit if any |
| Do I need a consultation first? Is it charged? How long is it? | consultation page: length, fee or free, what happens, what to bring; consultation request (Plus) |
| Home trays or in-clinic? What's the difference? | whitening page: the two (or the clinic's systems) side by side in the clinician's words; treatment journey (Plus) |
| How many visits, over how long? | treatment journey (Plus): visits, gaps and what happens at each, as the clinic sets them; "your clinician confirms your plan" |
| Will it hurt? What about sensitivity? | whitening page and aftercare: the clinician's own wording on sensitivity and what they recommend |
| How white will my teeth go? Can you show me? | consultation only; the site promises no shade. Smile gallery (Signature): consented before/after photographs, unedited, each with the treatment and clinician named |
| Will my crowns, veneers or fillings whiten too? | whitening page and journal, the clinician's words (they don't change colour; the clinic says what it offers instead) |
| How long will it last? Can I keep it up? | aftercare page: top-ups, what stains, the clinic's own top-up gel price |
| I have a wedding or event: when should I start? | wedding smile timeline (Signature): enter the date, see when to book each step, worked back from the clinic's own timings; add to calendar |
| Can I have it if I'm under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding? | patient information: under-18 rule from the GDC statement; anything else only in the clinician's words |
| Do you do bonding, veneers, aligners, hygiene? | treatments and prices; a page per treatment the clinic offers (Signature) |
| Is it NHS or private? | footer and patient information (GDC advertising guidance asks practices to say NHS, mixed or private) |
| How do I complain? Who regulates you? | complaints and patient information page: the clinic's procedure, then the Dental Complaints Service for private care, the GDC's address and link (every plan) |
| Can I arrive privately? Is there a waiting room? Parking, step-free access? | discreet arrival (Signature); Plan your visit (every plan) |
| Can I pay in instalments? Is there a deposit? | prices page: only what the clinic offers (its own finance partner link, its Stripe deposit link) |
| What happens after? Who do I call if something feels wrong? | aftercare page (Plus): the clinic's own instructions and out-of-hours line |
| Will my photos or details be shared? | privacy notice (every site) and the consent line on every gallery photo |

**Sources by type (no names quoted on any page):** private clinic FAQ pages on whitening, sensitivity and longevity;
whitening-vs-shop-kit comparison pages; clinic booking pages; patient reviews on Google and doctor directories; GDC
patient advice on checking the register. Regulator sources and dates: see `playbook.md` (UK rules).
