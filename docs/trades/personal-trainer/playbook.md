# Personal trainers and small gyms: playbook

What to build for this trade (no kit yet). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## The plan (from the original trade list)

8. PERSONAL TRAINERS AND SMALL GYMS. Demo: fictional
   Customers ask: Is this right for my goal? Where do we train? How much, packages?
   Qualified and insured? Results from people like me?
   Core: goals; prices and packs; questions; reviews; real qualifications only.
   Plus: class timetable from a Sheet with spaces left; results stories with written
   consent; online coaching page.
   SIGNATURE, "Your first 4 weeks":
   - a goal quiz (goal, experience, days per week, injuries) that suggests the right
     package with a sample week;
   - a session-pack builder with price per session;
   - "Book a free consult" on WhatsApp with their answers.
   Extras: a class "spaces left" counter; a referral card.
