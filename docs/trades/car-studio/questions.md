## Car studio (tint, wrap, PPF, detailing)
Legal facts come from gov.uk (tinted vehicle window rules, checked 26 Oct 2026): first used on or after
1 April 1985, windscreen at least 75% light through and front side windows at least 70%; before that date,
both at least 70%; no rules for the rear windows. The law is about the light through the glass as fitted, so
studios give shades as they measure them on the car, never as the film's own rating.
