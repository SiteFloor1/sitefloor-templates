# Car studios (tint, wrap, PPF, detailing): playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `car`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## The plan (from the original trade list)

1. CAR STUDIOS (tint, wrap, PPF, detailing). Demo: "Apex Studio", Birmingham
   Customers ask: Have you done my car? Is this tint legal? How much? How long, can I wait?
   Warranty? Can I see the colour first?
   Core: price list with "from" prices, Our work filtered by make (#bmw, #tesla),
   before/after slider, questions.
   Plus: services pages (tint, wrap, PPF, detailing), warranty page, booking with deposit
   (Stripe Payment Link).
   SIGNATURE, "The Showroom":
   - a drawn side and front view of the customer's body type (hatch, saloon, SUV, van);
   - they try window tint shades and see the result through the glass;
   - a wrap colour or finish (gloss, satin, matte, chrome), wheel colours and a de-chrome toggle;
   - a live price and time estimate;
   - a UK legality check: windscreen at least 75% light through, front side windows at
     least 70%; mark illegal choices clearly (check the current rule before shipping);
   - "Send this build" opens WhatsApp with the full spec.
   Extras: a "Have you done my model?" search over tagged work; a ceramic-coating care
   reminder card.
