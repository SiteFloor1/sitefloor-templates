# Bridal salons: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `bridal`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Luxury bridal salon (new `bridal` kit) — directions stage, 29 Oct 2026
- Customer: a bride with a budget of several thousand pounds, often 9–14 months out, coming with up to three people.
  The site's job: get the right appointment request with the date, party size and designers in it.
- Demo brand **Wexley** (fictional; searched, no UK salon uses it), Chelsea, by appointment. Three directions and their
  one-screen mockups: `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/bridal-directions.md`. Owner picked **Dove** (29 Oct) and the kit is built: look
  `dove`, demo `sites/demos/bridal`, contract `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/wexley.md`.
- **Starter:** `appointment` (the card and the request with date, party size, when, designers), `designers`.
- **Plus:** `gowns-page` (finder; two or three gowns full screen on home), `trunk-shows` (Google Form waiting list),
  `fittings-page` (fittings, alterations, veils), `brides-page` (reviews as quotes, photos), `gift-page` (vouchers).
- **Signature:** `gown-diary` (order-by date first, then every step, .ics, WhatsApp), `gown-mirror`, `shortlist` (link-only, nothing stored).
- **Alternatives (not built):** veil lengths on one gown; the sample rail live from a Sheet with a deposit link per
  gown; "Your gown, live" (the car kit's tracker logic); the appointment hour by hour.
- Honesty: requests, never "booked"; fees, lead times, fittings, prices and designers come from the client; no real
  designer names or official campaign photos in the demo.
