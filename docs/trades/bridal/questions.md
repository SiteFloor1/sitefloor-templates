# Bridal salons: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `bridal`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Luxury bridal salon (bridal kit, branch `trade/bridal`)
Researched 29 Oct 2026 from real UK salons' own pages (Phillipa Lepley, La Novia, Sassi Holford, Emilia Wickstead,
Browns Bride listings, a Vivienne Westwood sample sale). Every number on a client site comes from the client.

| Customers ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Do I need an appointment? Is there a fee, and is it taken off the gown? | `appointment` card (by appointment, fee and whether it's redeemable, cancellation notice) |
| How long is the appointment and how many people can I bring? | `appointment` card (length, guests); party size in the request |
| What should I bring or wear? | `appointment` card ("bring" line: shoes of the right height, underwear) |
| What do your gowns cost? | price band per designer and "from" per gown (client's own figures, or "price on request") |
| Which designers do you stock? Can you get in a gown I've seen? | `designers` list with what each is known for; "ask for a designer" field in the request |
| How far ahead must I order? Am I too late? | order-by calculator (Plus) / gown diary (Signature) from the salon's own lead times |
| How many fittings, when, and are alterations included? | fittings and alterations page (number, timing, what's included, extra cost) |
| Do you hold trunk shows or sample sales? Can I join the list? | `events` from a Sheet (dates, designer, sizes); waiting list by Google Form |
| Do you sell veils, headpieces and accessories? | veils and accessories page; veil lengths shown on real photos |
| What sizes are the samples? Can I try on if I'm not a sample size? | `appointment` FAQ in the salon's words |
| Can I bring my bridesmaids or mother? Do you dress them? | party-size request; bridesmaids and mothers page if the salon does it |
| Where are you, is there parking, which station? | Plan your visit (address, nearest station, parking, step-free access, hours) |
| What deposit, and can I cancel? | order terms in the salon's words (deposit share, final balance, refunds) |
| Can I take photos at the appointment? | `appointment` FAQ (many salons say no; video call for absent family) |
