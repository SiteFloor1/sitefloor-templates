# Independent tour operators: customer questions

What people ask a small, tailor-made tour operator before they book, in the order they ask it. Gathered from the
public FAQ, "when to go" and booking-terms pages of UK tailor-made operators (the VISION references and their peers)
and from the questions a planner hears on a first call. Every answer comes from the client; nothing here is a fact
the site states on its own. "Where it's answered" names the approved feature (VISION: TOUR-1 to TOUR-9) or page.

## Dreaming: "could we go?"

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| Where should we go in October (or at Easter, or over half-term)? | When to go (TOUR-4); the trip finder (TOUR-3) |
| What's the weather like there in March? | Weather at destination (TOUR-7): "typical", from a named source, never a forecast passed off as fact |
| Is it too hot / too wet / too busy then? | When to go: typical highs and rain from the source; "busy" only in the client's own words |
| We have ten days and two children. What fits? | Trip finder (TOUR-3): when, who, how long |
| What does a day actually look like? How much time is spent driving or flying? | Sample itinerary with map (TOUR-5); Journeys pages |
| Can we combine two places (Chile and Argentina, safari and beach)? | Journeys; trip builder (TOUR-6) |
| Is it suitable for a honeymoon / young children / an older parent / someone who can't walk far? | Honeymoons, Family travel; each journey's "suits" line (client's words) |

## Price and what's included

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| Roughly what does it cost? Per person or in total? | Trips (TOUR-1): "from" prices as the client publishes them, per person, with the basis stated |
| Are flights included? Which airports can we fly from? | Each journey's "included" list; ATOL and booking terms |
| What's included: guides, transfers, meals, park fees, tips? | Each journey's "included" list (client's words) |
| What deposit do you take and when is the balance due? | ATOL and booking terms (client's terms, linked or quoted exactly) |
| Can we pay in instalments? | ATOL and booking terms |
| Why book with you rather than direct with the lodge? | Travel experts; the client's own "how we work" |

## Trust: "is our money safe?"

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| Are you ATOL protected? What's your ATOL number? | ATOL number (TOUR-2): a client-supplied field, shown exactly as the client gives it, in the footer and on the terms page |
| Will I get an ATOL certificate? What if you go bust? | ATOL and booking terms: the client's own wording, linked to the CAA's public pages, never paraphrased by us |
| Are you a member of ABTA / another body? | Only if the client says so, with their membership number; never assumed |
| Has anyone here actually been? Who will plan our trip? | Travel experts: each person, where they've been (client's words), direct line |
| What do other travellers say? | Reviews: real, copied with name and date (shared feature) |
| What happens if something goes wrong while we're away? | ATOL and booking terms; the client's 24-hour number if they have one |

## Rules and getting ready

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| Do we need a visa? How long must my passport be valid? | Travel-ready checklist (TOUR-8): links to the gov.uk foreign travel advice page for each country; never restated as fact |
| Is it safe to travel there now? | Travel-ready checklist: the gov.uk foreign travel advice link, prominently; no summary of our own |
| Do we need vaccinations? | Travel-ready checklist: link to the official health advice the gov.uk page points to; the client's words only |
| Do I need travel insurance? | Booking terms (client's requirement) |
| What should we pack? How warm is it at night? | Journal; travel-ready checklist (client's list); typical temperatures from the named source |
| What's the time difference? Which plugs? | Destination pages (factual, checked) |

## Booking

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| Is there still space in March? How far ahead must we book? | Enquire; the client's lead-time note per journey |
| Can we talk it through first? Video call or in person? | Enquire: call, WhatsApp, or a request for a time |
| Can you change the itinerary? | Every journey ("a starting point"); trip builder (TOUR-6) |
| Can we see our trip in one place before we pay? | Proposed: private trip page by code (see the design round) |
