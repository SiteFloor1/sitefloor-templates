# Day nurseries and pre-schools: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `nursery`, not built yet). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Day nursery and pre-school (design round 1, branch `trade/nursery`)
Written 26 Nov 2026 from what parents ask on real UK nurseries' fees, FAQ and "book a visit" pages and nursery listings
(daynurseries.co.uk), and from gov.uk's funded-childcare pages. The customer is the parent; the child is from a few months
old to school age. Every fee, room, ratio, menu, policy and date on a client site comes from the client. The Ofsted
outcome and report link are shown only as the nursery supplies them, in the report's own words (Ofsted now publishes
report cards). Funding wording is linked to gov.uk with the date it was checked; we never decide a family's eligibility.

Funding facts checked on gov.uk, 26 Nov 2026 (England):
- Free Childcare for Working Parents: up to 30 hours a week for 38 weeks a year, from the term after the child turns
  9 months until school, for eligible working families; families reconfirm every 3 months
  ([gov.uk/free-childcare-if-working](https://www.gov.uk/free-childcare-if-working)).
- 15 hours a week for every 3 and 4 year old, from the term after their third birthday; 15 hours for some 2 year olds
  whose families get certain support ([gov.uk/help-with-childcare-costs](https://www.gov.uk/help-with-childcare-costs)).
- The DfE statutory guidance (valid from 1 April 2026) lets nurseries charge for meals, consumables, activities and
  extra hours, but those charges must be voluntary for the funded hours, and invoices are broken down into funded
  hours, paid hours, food, non-food consumables and activities
  ([gov.uk statutory guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/early-education-and-childcare--2)).
  So a fees tool shows funded hours as free and every extra as optional, never folded into a "funded" price.

| Parents ask | What answers it |
|---|---|
| Do you have a place for my child? Which room, and from when? | NUR-1 places by age room (Starter): age tapped, the room, places now and next term, from the nursery's sheet |
| How much is it? Per day, per session, per month? What's included? | NUR-2 fees (Starter): the nursery's own rates; the headline includes every mandatory fee (CMA) |
| What do the funded hours actually save me? Do I still pay for meals and nappies? | "Your week" fees worked out (Signature): days tapped, funding chosen, funded hours taken off, extras shown as optional |
| When does my funding start? My baby turns 9 months in… | "When funding starts" (new): date of birth in, the term from gov.uk's "term after" rule, linked |
| Are you term-time only or all year? Can funded hours be stretched? | Fees page, in the nursery's words; the worked-out fees show stretched or term-time as the nursery offers |
| Can I come and look round? When? | NUR-3 visit request (Plus): times the nursery offers, a WhatsApp or email message |
| What does a day look like? Naps, meals, outside time? | NUR-4 a day at nursery (Plus): the nursery's own timings |
| What will they eat? Do you cater for allergies, vegetarian, halal? | NUR-5 weekly menu (Plus) and the nursery's allergy statement |
| What's your Ofsted outcome? Can I read the report? | NUR-8 Ofsted report linked (Plus): as the nursery supplies it, linked to reports.ofsted.gov.uk |
| How many staff per child? Who will look after my baby? Key person? | Rooms and Our team pages: ratios only as the nursery states them |
| How does settling in work? How long? Can I stay? | NUR-7 settling-in planner (Signature): the nursery's own steps as dated sessions |
| When are you closed? Bank holidays, Christmas, training days? | NUR-6 term dates and closures (Signature): one calendar file |
| Opening hours, late pick-up, what if I'm late? | Plan your visit and the fees page, in the nursery's words |
| Do you have a garden? How much time outside? What if it rains? | Outdoor learning page |
| Is there a waiting list? Do I pay a deposit or registration fee? | NUR-1 (a full room becomes a waiting-list message) and the fees page, figures from the nursery |
| My child has additional needs or allergies. Can you support them? | Questions, in the nursery's words, and a private WhatsApp or email question |
| Do you take Tax-Free Childcare and childcare vouchers? | Fees page, in the nursery's words, linked to gov.uk |
| Where is it? Parking, buggy storage, drop-off? | Plan your visit |
