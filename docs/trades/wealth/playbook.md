# Wealth managers: playbook

What to build for this trade (kit `wealth`). The ground rules and the steps for a new kit: [`docs/TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md`](../../TRADE-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Private wealth management (kit `wealth`, look `foreshore`, demo Lyall Harrow), 4 Nov 2026 (PR #63)
- **Design process:** four rounds (`sitefloor-internal/docs/design/wealth.md`): the owner approved a calm white screen in light Mona Sans,
  one navy band, copper only as a line or link, people at a distance in one warm daylight grade. Handover `sitefloor-internal/docs/HANDOVER-WEALTH.md`.
- **FCA rules shape the kit:** no performance figures, return claims or calculators (the build refuses promise words in
  any setting, and the insights sheet holds such rows back); capital-at-risk wording beside the FCA line (COBS 4.2.4G);
  what the FCA doesn't regulate said plainly (COBS 4.2.4(4)); insights always "general information, not advice"; the
  FRN and each adviser's IRN from the firm, noughts on a demo.
- **Always on:** `fca` (the FCA line, Regulatory information page) and `check` (Is this really us?).
- **Starter:** the situation chooser, how we help (with the firm's minimum), your advisers with IRNs, the office band
  with its time now, Regulatory information and Is this really us? (free pages).
- **Plus:** insights published from the firm's own Google Sheet once compliance ticks them (home and page), how we work
  and fees, the people page, Arrange a meeting (refuses account numbers) with What to bring, each adviser's own page
  (photo, what they look after, FCA register link, a booking link that picks them in the meeting form), events from the
  firm's sheet (seminars and next-generation evenings; approved rows only, past ones drop off, reply and calendar file),
  the letter-of-wishes guide (general information, every section sourced).
- **Signature (16 real pages; the bar is "would a client show this to someone?"):** three showpieces: a page for each
  moment (selling a business, family and succession, moving to the UK, retiring, giving) with the timeline; the
  documents checklist per moment (exactly what to gather, tick, print or copy; no figures, no advice); the family map
  (on the device only). Plus discreet offices.
- **Easy extras for lower tiers:** Starter gets "Is this really us?" (check a call or email) free; Plus gets What to
  bring and the letter-of-wishes guide.
- **Not offered:** reviews, gallery, vouchers, the shared team page, questions, Plan your visit (the meeting and offices
  pages do its job), seasonal, photo band, page links.
- **Alternative showpieces (pitch to clients):** offices on a world map with local times for international families;
  a "your first year with us" page drawn from the firm's own onboarding steps; a next-generation reading list the firm
  publishes from its sheet with the same compliance tick; a quiet client-login link page that warns about fraud first.
