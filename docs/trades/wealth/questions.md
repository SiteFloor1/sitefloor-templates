# Wealth managers: customer questions

What people ask a private wealth manager before a first meeting, from UK firms' public pages (UBS UK, J.P. Morgan
Private Bank London, Rothschild & Co Wealth Management, Cazenove Capital, Coutts' fee tariff) and the FCA's rules
(checked Nov 2026). Where the kit answers each:

| Question | Where it's answered |
|---|---|
| Do you work with people like me (business owners, families, people moving to the UK, trustees)? | How we help (`help.audience`), the situation chooser, the moments' pages |
| What is your minimum? | How we help (`help.minimum`, the firm's own figure) |
| Who will I actually deal with, and can I reach them directly? | Your advisers (people page: direct lines, what each leads); each adviser's own page with a booking link |
| Are you regulated? What is your FCA number, and can I check it? | The FCA line under the hero and on every page; Regulatory information; each adviser's IRN |
| How are you paid? What will it cost me in pounds? | How we work: fees, in pounds, in plain English |
| Discretionary or advisory? Pensions, trusts, lending? | How we work (steps), the moments' pages |
| Will you work with my solicitor and accountant? | The moments' pages and the timeline (three lanes) |
| I'm selling my business / someone has died / we're moving to Britain: when should I talk to you? | The chooser's next step; the moment's page |
| How often will we meet? | How we work (yearly review) |
| Can my children be involved? | Family and succession; insights; next-generation evenings (Events) |
| Do you hold seminars? Can I bring a guest? | Events: from the firm's sheet, approved by compliance, reply and calendar file |
| What papers will you need from me for a sale, a move or retirement? | The documents checklist per moment (tick, print, copy) |
| What is a letter of wishes, and do I need one? | The letter-of-wishes guide (general information, sourced; your solicitor writes it) |
| How discreet are you? Where do we meet; can you come to us? | The office band; Discreet offices (entrance, parking, step-free, we come to you) |
| What happens at the first meeting, and what should I bring? | Arrange a meeting; What to bring |
| How do I know a call or email is really from you? | Is this really us? (always on) |
| How do I complain? | Regulatory information: the firm, then the Financial Ombudsman Service |
