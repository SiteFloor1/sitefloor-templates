Taken by Ambry: the stem-white ground with a pale wash of the week's colour, the week's colour itself as the accent
(sampled from the flowers each week), Hedvig Letters Serif and Sans, the pressed seal monogram, flowers edge to edge
over a band in the week's colour, the wrap in the week's colour. A second look on this kit, for Instagram and TikTok
bouquet florists, is planned and must not reuse any of these. Design record: [`sitefloor-internal/docs/design/florist.md`](https://github.com/SiteFloor1/sitefloor-internal/blob/main/docs/design/florist.md).

Taken by Laleh: the home page as the florist's feed (ranges over a three-column 4:5 grid with a pinned post two by
two), the drop as one post with a countdown, "Ask for this one" in italic under each post, Tenor Sans in normal case
with Spectral, the pearl pin (wordmark, Pinned flag, holding each card), one satin-ribbon divider, the handwritten-style
card, the warm white page with a peony-petal accent on text and lines only. Design record:
[`sitefloor-internal/docs/design/laleh.md`](https://github.com/SiteFloor1/sitefloor-internal/blob/main/docs/design/laleh.md).
