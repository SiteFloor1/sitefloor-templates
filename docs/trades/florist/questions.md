# Florists: customer questions

What this trade's customers ask before they get in touch (kit `florist`). How to read it and the questions every trade gets: [`docs/CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md`](../../CUSTOMER-QUESTIONS.md).

## Florist
From UK florists' own delivery pages and FAQs (McQueens, Grace & Thorn, Moyses Stevens, Appleyard London), Oct 2026.
| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Can you deliver today to my postcode? What's the cut-off? | `same-day`: the florist's own cut-offs and districts, a countdown, the next delivery day after it, a postcode check |
| Which days do you deliver? Bank holidays? | `same-day` (closed dates, bank holidays when closed); the footer's delivery days |
| What if nobody's in? How much is delivery? | questions (the florist's own policy and fee) |
| What's good for a birthday, thank you, funeral? Up to a budget? | `shop-by-occasion` with budgets; `sympathy-order` for funerals |
| Can I add a card? What should I write? | `card-composer` (live preview, their length); sympathy card lines |
| Will it look like the photo? What's in season? | `this-week` (the actual arrangement); `in-season` |
| How long will they last? Care? | `care-card` (QR on the card); questions |
| Do you do weekly flowers? Can I pause or skip? | `every-week` |
| Weddings: how far ahead? Is my date free? Can you match our colours? | `weddings-planner` (date check, palette from a swatch on WhatsApp) |
| Do you run workshops? Places left? | `workshops` |
| Can I remember birthdays? Give flowers for weeks? | `remember-date`, `gift-a-season` |
| Can I collect? Where do I park? | Plan your visit |

## Social-first bouquet florists (the kit's second look)
Their customers find them on Instagram or TikTok, usually on a phone, often the same day they need the flowers. They
ask in DMs, so the site's job is to answer the DM before it's sent. From the florists' own posts and order pages and the
references in [`playbook.md`](playbook.md#second-look-social-first-bouquet-florists), Nov 2026.

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What bouquets have you got today, and how much? | today's bouquets (live list with prices and "sold out") |
| Can you deliver today to my postcode? What time do I need to order by? | same-day line, postcode check, delivery areas (built) |
| Is the drop still on / is it sold out? When's the next one? | the drop (live count from the Sheet, the next drop time) |
| What wrap colours do you have? Can I pick the ribbon? | wraps and ribbons (real photos, names) in the builder and on each bouquet |
| Can you do this one from TikTok / Instagram / Pinterest? | "make this one": paste the link, a budget and a date, sent as one message |
| How much for a bouquet like this in my budget? | build your bouquet (real stems and wraps, priced from the florist's own list) |
| Can I add a card / balloon / chocolates? What will the card say? | card preview (built), gifts to add (built) |
| Can I collect? Where are you? (home studios) | collection: the area only on the site, the exact address in the reply |
| Do you do Valentine's / Mother's Day? When do pre-orders close? | pre-order windows (built) |
| Has it been delivered? Can I see it? | follow your order with the photo before it left (built) |
| Do I order here or in the DMs? How do I pay? | one order button: WhatsApp with the order written out, or Instagram with it copied; the florist's own Payment Link |
