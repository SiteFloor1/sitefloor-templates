# Event designers and producers: customer questions

What people ask an agency that designs and produces events (tablescapes, flowers, lighting, staging) before they
enquire. Most come from couples and families planning weddings and celebrations, then companies planning launches,
dinners and parties. The site should answer these before anyone has to message.

## "Are you free?"
- Are you available on our date? Can you hold it while we decide?
- How far ahead do people usually book you? Is next month too late?
- Do you take more than one event on the same day or weekend?
- We haven't fixed a date yet. Can we still talk?

## "Do you do…?"
- Weddings, including multi-day weddings (mehndi, nikah, reception)?
- Corporate dinners, brand launches, product reveals, Christmas parties?
- Birthdays, anniversaries, private dinners at home?
- Destination events, or only in the UK? Which parts of the UK?
- Design only, or design and production (build, lighting, staff on the day)?
- Flowers, furniture, linens, tableware, lighting, staging, AV: your own, or hired in?
- Can you work in our venue, or only venues you know? Can you help us find one?
- Small events (under 40 guests) as well as large ones?

## Money
- What does it cost? Is there a minimum spend?
- How do you charge: a design fee, a percentage, or a fixed quote?
- What would our budget realistically get us for this many guests?
- How much is the deposit, when is the rest due, and what happens if we cancel or move the date?
- Is VAT included in what you quote?

## What it will look like
- Can I see whole events, not just close-ups? Rooms before guests arrive?
- What colours and flowers work in the season we've chosen?
- Do we get a mood board, sketches or a plan of the room before we commit?
- Will there be a tasting, a mock-up table or a site visit?
- How will the room be laid out for our number of guests?

## The day itself
- What does the day look like hour by hour? When do you arrive and when do you leave?
- Who is our contact on the day?
- How long do you need in the venue to set up and clear down?
- What do you need from the venue (power, access, load-in times, curfews)?

## Trust
- Who have you worked with? Can we speak to a past client? (Only real, permitted references.)
- Are you insured? (Only the cover the client actually holds; never stated for them.)
- What happens if something goes wrong on the day, or a supplier lets us down?
- What happens to the flowers and materials afterwards?

## Rules and paperwork
- Who handles venue rules: fire safety, candles and open flames, noise limits, alcohol and music licences?
  (The venue and its licence decide; the site never states the law for a venue.)
- Do you need anything from us in writing (contract, guest numbers, final details by a set date)?
