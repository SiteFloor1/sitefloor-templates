# Demo map

Every Sitefloor demo, what it looks and feels like, and what is therefore taken. Read this before proposing any new
look: a new demo must not match any row on ground, palette source, signature colour, type, hero idea, header, phone bar
or case-study metaphor. When your demo is approved, add its row and what it takes to your own `docs/trades/<trade>/demo-map.md` and run `npm run docs`;
this file is generated from those files. Facts below are from
each look's `look.json`, `look.css` and README on `main`, 28 Oct 2026.

## What is taken (don't reuse), up to 3 Nov 2026
From then on each trade lists what its demos take in its own section below.

- **Grounds and moods:** near-black warm (Fade District); dark bottle green candlelit (Saffron & Ash); roasted-cocoa
  dark (Velvet Spoon); cool basalt grey, photograph-led (Isotach); pink-silver pearl daylight (Aldous Marr); warm cream with gilt and a candlelit evening (Grenat); warm paper (Swift Cuts); oat daylight
  (Morrow); cool white studio cyclorama (Apex Studio); pure white and ivory daylight salon (Wexley); bone-and-sand morning sun (Ottoline); mid-light Portland stone with sepia photographs (Calder Wyke); warm egg-tan stone in daylight with one umber band (Verrall). The owner calls Grenat and Velvet Spoon the "dark, warm luxury"
  pair: no new demo may feel like them.
- **Signature colours:** gold, enamel blue, signal red, saffron, paprika, tile teal, garnet, pistachio, wet-rock steel, dove violet, conch pink, carved-shadow blue-grey, sea-glass (and the polish-colour chip), dune shadow brown.
- **Fonts (each look's display, body and accent must be unique; `npm run rules` checks):** Playfair Display, Manrope,
  Bricolage Grotesque, Figtree, Hanken Grotesk, Cormorant Garamond, Jost, Bodoni Moda, Schibsted Grotesk, Gloock, Onest,
  Libre Caslon Display, Libre Caslon Text, DM Serif Display, Archivo, Host Grotesk, Rethink Sans, Gilda Display, Mulish, Italiana, Albert Sans, Noto Serif Display, Red Hat Text, Marcellus, Source Sans 3, Old Standard TT, Plus Jakarta Sans. Banned everywhere: Inter, DM Sans, Poppins,
  Montserrat. Sitefloor's own Manrope belongs to Sitefloor; no look uses Sitefloor's paper, ink or tomato.
- **Hero ideas:** stacked headline + barber pole; street plate + queue ticket; car on a white cyclorama; a book on a
  table over fire; a menu card; a photo in an arch with live tags; a room in a gilt frame; a full-screen pour under a
  ribbon; a full-bleed campaign photo of a person moving with one drop line; a gown in a three-way mirror; a reformer photograph with today's classes and their reformers along its foot; full-screen
  editorial gowns with a credit; one still macro piece with no headline; one carved sentence beside a tall interior photograph with the SRA badge under the call to action; one line over a hand, a shade card overlapping it, a swatch rail. Never reuse a hero composition.
- **Packaging motifs:** the barber pole, the street plate, the Menu Book and polaroids, the brass arch, the gilt frame
  and the stand, the ribbon, the seal and the chocolate box, the lookbook as a printed book, the fitting ticket, the six-reformer mark and the punched class card, the blind-embossed initials seal and the regulation plate, the shade card and the flat swatch.
- **Case-study metaphors:** spec sheet, tasting menu, three-tier stand, the box; Swift Cuts and Fade District each have
  their own. Never mimic an earlier case study's structure either (the owner's instruction for Velvet Spoon).

Links to every published demo and case study are in each handover's "Published links".
