# Move-out planner (cleaning kit, Signature)

On the end-of-tenancy page: a check-out date gives the day of the clean (`tenancy.cleanDaysBefore`) and the day to
book by (`tenancy.bookDaysBefore` before the clean), both in UK order, to the calendar (an all-day reminder) or sent on
WhatsApp with `tenancy.message`. Too late or too soon is said plainly; nothing promises a slot.
