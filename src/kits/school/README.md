# School kit (`school`)

For small UK independent prep and senior schools. Not built yet: design round 1 is with the owner; only the admissions
logic exists (`lib/admissions.js`, tests in `tests/unit/school-admissions.test.js`).

## Rules for every client site (non-negotiable)
1. **Safeguarding: no identifiable child's face, anywhere.** Not in the school's photographs, film, Instagram feed
   embeds or stock. Use buildings, grounds, classrooms, hands, objects, backs and distance. No child's name next to
   any image. The school confirms this for every image it supplies; when in doubt, leave the image out.
2. **Fees exactly as the school publishes them**, per term or per year as it says, **including VAT** (20% on
   education and boarding since 1 January 2025; nursery classes of children under compulsory school age stay exempt,
   so show those as the school states them), with the date the figures were checked. The kit never works VAT out.
3. **Never invent bursary or scholarship terms.** Use the school's words and link its policy.
4. **Inspection (SCH-8): link the real ISI or Ofsted report.** Never quote selectively, never paraphrase an outcome.
5. **Results only as the school publishes them, with the year.** No league-table or ranking claims.
6. **Admissions dates come from the school.** SCH-5 works out the year group from the date of birth (England and
   Wales, 1 September cut-off) and counts the school's own deadlines; it never assumes a deadline.
