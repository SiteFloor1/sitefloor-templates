# Extension visualiser page (construction kit, Signature)

The visualiser on its own page (`extension.html`), larger, with two things the home teaser doesn't have: a price range
from the builder's own rates and a button that sends the design (house type, size and roof in plain words) on
WhatsApp. With no `visualiser.rates`, no price is shown at all (a demo says where it would show). Rates are the
builder's own £ per m² of new room, low and high, per roof, with VAT stated and the month checked; the range is rounded
out to the nearest £1,000 and called "a range, not a quote".
