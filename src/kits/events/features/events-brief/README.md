# events-brief (Plus)

Start your event (`start.html`, EVT-2): a short form, not a questionnaire: occasion (`brief.types`, the agency's
words), date or "not fixed" with "we could move it", guests, the agency's own budget bands (`brief.budgets`; leave out
to not ask), venue (`brief.venueChoices`), name and a note. The message is shown as it's written and opens on WhatsApp,
or by email to `brief.email` (else the site's contact email). Nothing is sent from the page and nothing is stored.

With `events-dates` on, the page also carries the date check, and the chosen date says free, held or taken from the
same diary; with `events-key-dates` on, the agency's milestones follow from the chosen date.
