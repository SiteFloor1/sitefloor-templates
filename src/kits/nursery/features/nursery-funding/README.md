## How it works
The fees page: every rate and optional extra, the nursery's words on funded hours with gov.uk linked, and "When can
funding start?": the child's date of birth in, the working families' hours (from the term after 9 months) and the
universal 15 hours (from the term after the third birthday) out. gov.uk: a child reaching the age 1 January to 31
March starts from 1 April; 1 April to 31 August, from 1 September; 1 September to 31 December, from 1 January.
"What a week costs" follows when it is on.

## Rules
It never says a family is eligible: it gives the term and links to gov.uk, where families apply for their code.
