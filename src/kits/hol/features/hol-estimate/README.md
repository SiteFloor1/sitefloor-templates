# hol-estimate: what could it earn? (HOL-6, Signature)

Under the hero photograph: one sum, what guests pay − the fee = what reaches the owner, a year. It comes ONLY from the
company's own figures for last year (`estimate.year`, `estimate.sizes`: for each size of home the average night with
every fee in, nights booked, how many homes, and the lowest and highest a home paid its owner). Never market data. The
fee is `fees.percent`, VAT included (and `estimate.percent` must match it). Without published figures the feature
can't be turned on, and the hero shows the plain question with a link to the revenue review instead.

Own weeks are taken off at the same rate as any other week of the year (`lib/estimate.js`, said on the page). With
`estimate.london: true` nights stop at 90 a year, linked to the Deregulation Act 2015, s.44. The basis, year, number of
homes and range are on the same screen as the figure, with "not a promise". Worked out at build time for the most
common size, so it reads the same without JavaScript.
