# hol-fees: what we do and what it costs (HOL-1, Starter, free page)

On the home page: the management fee as a percentage of what guests pay with VAT included (`fees.percent`,
`fees.vatIncluded` must be true), worked in pounds on `fees.example`, then who does what (`fees.weDo`, `fees.youKeep`).
The fees page adds every other charge once (`fees.charges`: name, when, amount in pounds with VAT, 0 for none), the
notice to leave (`fees.notice`) and the office (`visit`). Free on every plan and in the menu of every page, so an owner
always sees the full cost before getting in touch. The same `fees.percent` drives the estimate and the statement.
