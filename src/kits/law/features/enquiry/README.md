# Speak to a partner (home)

Choose what it's about; the partner who leads that practice is shown with a direct line and email. Without scripts every area and partner is listed.

## Config

Uses `practice.items[].lead` and that person's `email` and `phone`. Heading from `sections.enquiry`.

Markup is neutral and styled with tokens; each look restyles it (and may override it in `src/looks/<look>/features/`).
