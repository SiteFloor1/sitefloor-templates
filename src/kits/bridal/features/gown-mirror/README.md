# gown-mirror (bridal kit, Signature)

"Every side" on a gown opens it in the mirror: three panes, as in a fitting room, its first photograph in the middle
and the next two angled in at either side, each captioned (Front, Back, The bodice…). Two photographs give two
panes; a gown with one photograph has no button. Uses the salon's own photographs only: `gowns.items[].views`, at
least two. Escape, Close or a tap outside closes it and returns focus to the gown.
