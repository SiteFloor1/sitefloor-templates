# textures (hair kit, Plus)

Every texture side by side: straight, waves, curls, coils and braids as tall photographs (the salon's own, or licensed
until then). Choose one and it widens; the line under the strip names who cuts it (from `stylists`), the price from
and the time (from the service on the menu, or "Priced at a free consultation"), and what to arrive with, with a
WhatsApp link for that service. Without JavaScript every texture's line shows.

```json
"textures": { "items": [{ "id": "curls", "name": "Curls", "photo": { "src": "img/texture-curls.webp", "width": 560, "height": 1120, "alt": "…" },
  "stylists": ["marcus", "ada"], "service": "curl-cut", "arrive": "Come with curls unbrushed and dry, and no product the day before." }] }
```
Ids are the five textures; photos are tall (1:2). Heading: `sections.textures`.
