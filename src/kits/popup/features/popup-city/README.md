# Bring us to your city (`popup-city`, Signature)

Under the waitlist: a city, sent as a pre-filled WhatsApp message (`message` must hold `{city}`), or a link to the
brand's own Google Form (`formUrl`). The site stores and counts nothing, and shows no counts.

```json
"city": { "title": "Not on the list? Ask for your city.", "message": "Hello Chalden, please bring the pop-up to {city}." }
```
