# work-stories (florist kit, Signature, pages `stories` and `story-<id>`)

Our work, as stories: a page per wedding or event with the brief in the florist's words, the palette (two to five
colours with their names, drawn as swatches), the stems, three or four of their photographs, the venue and the month.
The index shows each story's first photograph. A collection: one page on the plan's count. Real events only on a
client site (the couple's or client's permission is the florist's to get); the demo's are illustrative.
