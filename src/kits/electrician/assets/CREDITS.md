# Electrician kit files

Shared by every electrician site (copied to `img/kit/`).

## The car in "The charger on your driveway" (ev-3d)

`ev-car.glb` is the car kit's studio car: "Car Concept" from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept), © 2024 Darmstadt Graphics Group
GmbH, model and textures by Eric Chadwick, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
prepared for Sitefloor as described in `src/kits/car/assets/CREDITS.md` (logos removed, meshopt, WebP textures). It
carries no maker's badge. The credit line shows on the 3D stage whenever the car is drawn; keep it there.

## The still before the 3D charger loads

`ev-still.webp` is a render of that same scene (Sitefloor's procedural house front with the car above), 1600×1000, so
the same CC BY 4.0 credit applies to the car in it.

## The stills of the house in 3D (house-3d)

`villa-wide.webp` (1600×900) and `villa-tall.webp` (900×1125) are renders of Sitefloor's own model in
`features/house-3d/villa-scene.js` (garden and pool lit, the house dark), made in a browser from that file. No third-party
material.
