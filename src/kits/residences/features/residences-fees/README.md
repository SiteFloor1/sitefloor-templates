# residences-fees: fees and terms (every plan, free page)

Required on every residences site. What a short stay includes and costs (`fees.stays`, `fees.charges`, the
cancellation terms), the tenancy rules (rolling tenancies priced by the month; only the payments the Tenant Fees Act
permits) linked to GOV.UK with the date checked (`lib/rules.js`), the operator's own tenancy lines (`fees.tenancy`),
and the redress scheme (`schemes.redress`, one of the two approved schemes; a demo shows 000000), optional client money
protection (`schemes.cmp`) and how deposits are protected (`schemes.deposits`). Nothing here is worked out: it says
what the operator supplies.
