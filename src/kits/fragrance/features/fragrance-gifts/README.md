# Gifts, engraving and wrapping (FRAG-4, Plus)

`gifts`: `title`, `intro?`, `wrap?` (`price`, `line`: the house's wrapping), `terms?` (delivery, returns for engraved
bottles), `message` (the WhatsApp order: `{fragrance}`, `{ml}`, `{price}` (the total), `{extras}` (engraving, wrapping,
the card)). Uses `engraving` for its price, letter limit, styles and places. The total is worked out from the house's
prices; the order goes on WhatsApp for the house to confirm and send its payment link. Its page: Gifts.
