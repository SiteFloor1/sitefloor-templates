# tourism-daylight (Plus)

Daylight on each journey page: choose a month (it opens on the journey's next good month) and every stop shows its
sunrise, sunset and hours of light on the 15th, in its own clock ("Sunrise 05:41 · sunset 21:12 · 15 h 31 min of
light"). Worked out in the browser from the stop's `lat`, `lon` and `tz` with NOAA's solar equations (to a minute or
two; inside the polar circles it says when the sun doesn't rise or set). No settings; nothing claimed about weather.
