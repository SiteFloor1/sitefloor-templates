# modest-length (Plus)

The size and length guide (MOD-3), its own page (`/length`): how to measure shoulder to floor (`lengths.measure`), then
her measurement and shoes give the shop's length ("Wear the 56. It stops 1″ above the floor.") and every piece that
comes in it, with its price; the shop's height chart beneath (`modest-size-guide`'s chart). Settings: `lengths`, as
in `modest-hem-line`. The pieces listed are those whose `lengths` include hers; no stock is claimed.
