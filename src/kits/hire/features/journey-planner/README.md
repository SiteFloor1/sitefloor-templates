# Feature: journey-planner (hire kit, Signature showpiece)

On the chauffeur page. **Plan:** Signature.

- **The day, stop by stop:** pick-up, ceremony, reception, drop-off (add or remove stops; at least two), each with the
  customer's own place and time.
- **Hours and the quote:** from the first time to the last (past midnight counts), rounded up to the client's billing
  step (`hire.chauffeur.stepMinutes`) and never below the car's minimum (`chauffeur.minHours`), at the car's hourly rate.
  **Never a guessed drive time:** the customer enters the times and the client confirms the route and the price.
- **The itinerary card:** the couple's names, the date, every stop and time, the car, in the house style; "Share the
  card with the party" uses the phone's share sheet (or copies the text).
- **The request:** WhatsApp to the business with the whole plan and the hours from the customer's times.
