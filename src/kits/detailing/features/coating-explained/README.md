## How it works
The section's line, then ceramic coating and paint protection film side by side: what each does and what it
doesn't, in the studio's words, beside one large photograph (on the demo, water beading on paint). The Ceramic
coating and Paint protection film pages show the same two lists. No figures of our own: how long a coating lasts or
what a film is warranted for are the studio's words, from its own products.
