## How it works
The front of a black gloss saloon, split down the middle: the left half is bare paint, the right half under film.
Tapping the car throws a stone from the road ahead to that spot. On the paint it leaves a chip through to the primer and
the counter goes up; on the film it ripples and leaves nothing. "Drive the motorway" throws sixteen stones at random
spots; "Wash it off" clears the chips. Only the body from the bonnet down can be hit (the picture's own outline,
read from its transparency), never the windscreen or the background. With reduced motion, stones land at once.

It is an illustration of what film is for, and the page says so: no thickness, warranty or impact figures, and it
admits a hard enough hit can mark the film. If the protection planner is on, a link goes to it.
