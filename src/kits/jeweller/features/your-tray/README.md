# your-tray (jeweller kit, Plus)

Your tray: "Set aside" beside any piece puts it on the customer's tray (up to three, as a jeweller lays out a tray for
a visit). A small tab shows how many; it opens the list with Remove and "Request an appointment to see them", which
sends the pieces on WhatsApp. The appointment request on the home page lists them too ("To have ready").

Kept in this browser only (localStorage `jw-tray`); nothing is sent until the customer sends the request. The tab is
hidden while the tray is empty. Needs `appointment-request`. Included on every page by the look's footer.
