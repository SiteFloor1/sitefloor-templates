# visit-prepared (jeweller kit, Starter)

What happens at an appointment, so a first visit to a fine jeweller holds no surprises: the house's own steps (e.g.
"You're met at the door", "Pieces on a tray", "Take your time", "Nothing to decide today"), how long it usually takes
and what to bring (a ring that fits, a photograph of a family piece). Config `prepared`: `steps` (`title`, `text`),
optional `length`, `bring`. Section copy: `sections.prepared`.
