# inside-the-band (jeweller kit, Signature)

Inside the band: the customer types the words (up to the house's own limit) and sees them follow the inside of a band
in the house's photograph as they type, in the house's lettering styles (script, roman or block); "Ask for this
engraving" sends the words and the style on WhatsApp. It's a preview of the words' place, not of the engraver's hand:
the house confirms what it can engrave. Shown on band pages (`band: true` or `engrave: true`) and the Wedding bands page.

Config `engraving`: `max` (letters), `styles` ([{ `id`: script|roman|block, `label` }]), `photo` (a plain band, inside
showing), `path` (an SVG path along the inside of the band, in the photo's pixels), `note` (the house's words: cost,
time, what it engraves).
