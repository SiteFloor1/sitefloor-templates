# piece-detail (lingerie kit, Signature)

A page for each piece (`piece-<id>`): its photographs (swipe on a phone), **Collection · Style · Colour**, the price,
its colours, its sizes and the fitters' note, and the actions: *Set it aside in my size* on WhatsApp (boutiques) or
*Buy on our shop* (`buyUrl`, online brands), with the tray and *Drop a hint* when those are on. Then the rest of the
collection. The same logic as the jeweller kit's piece pages (shared when both are on main); this markup is the
lingerie kit's own.
