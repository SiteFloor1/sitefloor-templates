# piece-pages (Signature) → `<piece id>.html`
A page for every piece (`pieces[].id` is its address, so ids can't be page names like "fit"): all its photographs, the
price, the stock words and each size in, few or out, Buy, its line; the fit block (as on the fit page); the lookbook
looks it appears in (linking into the book); the other pieces. Pages are made by pagination over `site.pieces`.
