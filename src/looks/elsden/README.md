# Look: Elsden (garage kit)

A gallery for cars, where the hall is the work. With the aerial hall (garage-aerial, a Bespoke add-on) the first
screen is the client's hall seen from straight above, edge to edge, its label resting on the forecourt like a
gallery card (`hero.kicker`, `title`, `where`, `medium`, `intro`, `from`) with the picker beneath; a car the size the
visitor picks drives in through the gate. Without it, the client's own photograph of the hall (`hero.photo`) under
the same label. Then the hall drawn to scale (Signature), spaces and prices, charging and cameras, arriving at night
on the same view after dark, how cars are kept hung like a show, and the rest. On a phone the picture comes first,
the gate at the bottom, then the picker and the label; the bar at the foot keeps the free bays and WhatsApp.

- Ground plaster `#E9E6E0`, paper `#F7F5F0`, ink `#1C1B19`; accent olive `#55613F` (a bay leaf), for the chosen bay
  and the picker only. Renders and photographs graded warm, to the hall's light.
- Type: Cardo (names, the label, headings), Radio Canada (everything else); size-matched fallbacks in `fonts.css`.
- Motion: one move, the car driving in (6.2 s, eased), then the bay lights; layers fade. Nothing is tied to scrolling;
  reduced motion sees the car parked.
- References: Jeffrey Milstein's straight-down aerials (the plan view, flat and ordered); Mini Motorways (one calm
  moving thing on a still top-down world; the motion only); a gallery wall label (the card); polished-concrete
  gallery rooms (the floor and the pools of light).
