# Spun (stalls kit)

A street-food stall that's joyful but well made. White page, ink words; the stall in action (the iron opening, the
pour) takes the right half of the first screen. One colour of our own, a ripe raspberry; prices in the crust's amber.

- **Hero:** where we are next (`stalls-where`, in `heroParts`): the place and day, counting down to opening, the week's
  stops as tickets crossing into the photograph. One short heavy line (`hero.title`, 14 characters a line at most).
- **Type:** Gabarito 600/700/800 for headings, prices and tickets; Labrada 400 for reading.
- **Home order:** where this week, menu, drop, posts, gallery, book us for your party, reviews, questions, visit.
- **Section openings:** the heading large and tight, its one line under it.
- **Header:** the mark, up to three page links, WhatsApp underlined in raspberry; Menu opens every page on a white sheet.
- **Phone bar:** "Where this week" and WhatsApp.
- **Footer:** the usual week as tickets, the stall's name large.

Design notes: `sitefloor-internal/docs/trades/stalls/design/directions.md`.
