# Booth

For the smile kit (teeth whitening and aesthetic dental studios). A young, founder-led studio, Instagram-first and
clinical where it matters.

- **Hero:** a photo-booth strip of four smiles (`hero.strip.frames`, exactly four, each with an optional `focus`
  such as `"50% 40%"`). Laid flat across the page on desktop; upright on a phone with landscape frames, sized so the
  whole strip stays within about one screen (90svh). Each frame develops in turn once after load; never with reduced
  motion. Beside the headline: the kicker, the intro and the lead clinician's register line at body size.
- **Type:** Reddit Sans 200/300/400 for headlines, facts and buttons; Brygada 1918 italic only for the clinician's
  own words (quotes, notes, the wordmark).
- **Colour:** white ground, warm black-and-white photographs, one lavender accent (links, the chosen tap, timelines);
  espresso for the wedding band and the footer.
- **Legal lines:** GDC numbers, the whitening and under-18 rules, "we don't promise a shade" and the complaints link
  are body size and full contrast everywhere (never small grey print).
- **Phone bar:** always on phones: the street on the left, "Book a consultation" filled on the right; the header's Book
  link hides there so it isn't shown twice.
- **Header and footer:** italic wordmark, light page links, Book underlined in lavender; an espresso footer with hours,
  pages, patient information, NHS or private, and the lead clinician's GDC number.
