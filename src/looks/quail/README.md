# Look: Quail (nails kit)

A hand and nail atelier in daylight. Demo: **Verrall** (`sites/demos/nail-salon`). The design contract, references and
the comparison with every other look are in `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/nail-salon.md`.

- **Palette from a quail's egg:** shell tan `#D7CABA` (page), the paler inside `#E8DFD3` (the card, the footer), the
  umber speckle `#2A211C` (text and the one deep band, the month's sets), the membrane's sea-green `#2F5A57` (links).
  The real colour is the polish: every Book is a chip in the shade on the hand.
- **Type:** Old Standard TT (names, headings; very large, italic for shade and set names) and Plus Jakarta Sans 400/600.
  Size-matched fallbacks in `fonts.css`.
- **Hero:** one confident line over the hand; the hand large, the shade card overlapping its edge like a jewellery tag;
  the next free time and Book under the card; the house shades as a rail of flat swatches beneath. On phones the card
  sits inside the photo's corner so its name never sits under the bottom bar.
- **Header:** "Menu" left (opens every page), the name in spaced capitals centred, Book right as a shade chip.
- **Section openings:** the name very large; its line beneath, set in from the left on the fourth column.
- **Home:** the hand and shades, the month's edit on the umber band, the menu by the hour, the salon (colour wall and
  a counter detail), the visit. `onPages` moves gel or BIAB, build your set, infills and reviews to the menu page, the
  finishes to the shades page, the bridal timeline and parties to the bridal page, vouchers to the gift page.
- **Footer:** the paler shell, the name very large in spaced capitals, address, hours, pages, the Sitefloor credit.
- **Phone bar:** stone; Call outlined, Book in the shade.
- **Buttons:** square chips; the only filled colour on the page is the polish.
