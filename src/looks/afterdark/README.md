# Look: After dark

For premium gyms and health clubs (kit `gym`). A members' training club after dark.

- **Hero:** one black-and-white portrait framed in the middle of the page; the club's count (gym-count: usual, or live
  from the door) in Epilogue 200 at 240–430px, crossing the portrait's left edge and turning to negative over it
  (`mix-blend-mode: difference`); under it the count's line, one sentence and one button. On phones the number crosses
  the portrait's foot.
- **Mark:** an H drawn with its crossbar rising like a slope (a halde is a slope), then the rest of the name in Epilogue
  300. A name not starting with H gets the H-slope emblem beside it; a client's own logo replaces both.
- **Second act:** heat and cold in colour (steam, cedar) against the black page.
- **Type:** Epilogue only (200 the count, 300 headings, 400 text, 500 small titles). Fallback metrics measured against
  Liberation Sans in `fonts.css`.
- **Palette:** black #0A0A0B, bone #F2F0EC, quiet grey #9A968F, river stone #CFC8BC for dates and quiet marks.
- **Never:** condensed capitals, tracked labels, yellow, pulsing dots, bars in the hero, motion tied to the scroll.
