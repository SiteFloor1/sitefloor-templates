# Basalt (wear kit)
A running and outerwear house after rain. Demo: Isotach (`sites/demos/menswear`).

- **Palette, from wet basalt:** ground #17191C, slab #1F2226, bone #E7E8E3 (text), ash #9A9E9A (secondary), wet-rock
  steel #A9B8C3 as the only accent (live states and link lines). No acid colours, no gold.
- **Type:** Host Grotesk 300 for names and the few large words; Rethink Sans 400/500 for everything else. Size-matched
  fallbacks in `fonts.css` (measured 29 Oct 2026). Almost no uppercase labels; the wordmark is the only spaced capitals.
- **Hero:** one full-bleed campaign photograph of the piece worn and moving; the piece's name at its foot and the next
  drop as one quiet line (`heroParts: ["drop-line"]`). `hero.photo` (16:9) and `hero.photoTall` (4:5, phones).
- **Section openings:** a light heading and a short line on the right; no small label above.
- **Header:** mark and spaced wordmark left; pages and "Ask us" right; over the hero on the home page.
- **Phone bar:** the next drop on the left, "Ask us" on the right.
- **Footer:** what's next (drop, run, showroom open or closed), then address, pages and the Sitefloor credit.
- **Ratios:** pieces 4:5, lookbook 3:4 portraits facing across the fold, wide bands 21:9 (4:5 on phones).
- **Motion:** the core's quiet fade-in; carousels and the book scroll by the visitor's hand. Nothing is tied to the page scroll.
- **Brand world:** emblem of three wind lines (isotachs on a weather chart); the lookbook as a printed book is the motif.
- **References:** On's campaign pages (one athlete mid-stride, the product name the only words), Satisfy (real weather,
  the club as part of the brand), Arc'teryx Veilance lookbooks (muted cool colour, very little copy), Dior Men show
  imagery (cinematic black and white, garments as large as runway looks).
