# Almanac (garden kit)

A gardener's almanac. Ivory and ink; the photographs carry the colour. A rose hip gives the accent: one word a screen
(`*word*` in a heading), the chosen month and focus. Low sun on stone gives `--sun-warm`, the top tone of the sun study.

- **Hero:** the year in twelve plants (`the-year`, in `heroParts`): tall photographs side by side, the visitor's month
  open with its name in italic across it. Under it one line: the studio's description (`hero.title`, the page's h1, set
  small) and `hero.intro` on the right.
- **Type:** Alegreya only, roman 400/500 and italic 400. Italic for months, labels, choices and plant names; no small
  capitals, no letter-spaced labels.
- **Home order:** the year, the studio's month, design/build/care, gardens (on the paler stone), a garden through the
  year, before/after, reviews, questions, visit.
- **Section openings:** an ink rule, the heading as a sentence under it, its one line in italic.
- **Phone bar:** ivory with a hairline; on the home page the month in italic ("November at the studio", to the studio's
  month, following the visitor's clock), elsewhere "The garden year"; "Start your garden" underlined in rose hip.
- **Footer:** ivory under an ink rule, the twelve month initials as a quiet line, the studio's name large in italic.
