# Look: Dune (pilates kit)

A reformer studio in morning sun. Demo: **Ottoline** (`sites/demos/pilates`). The design contract, references and
the comparison with every other look are in `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/pilates.md`.

- **Palette from a sun-baked dune:** its sunlit face, bone `#F4E8D8` (page); the lit crest `#FBF4EA` (cards, the
  footer, the phone bar); the shadow side `#8A5D40` (underlines, free reformers, step numbers; AA on both grounds);
  wet sand at the waterline `#2B2019` (text). Photographs carry one sunlit grade: midtones opened to the same light,
  warm shadows, clean highlights.
- **Type:** Noto Serif Display 200/300 and 300 italic (the name, headings, times) against Red Hat Text 400/500.
  Size-matched fallbacks in `fonts.css`.
- **The mark:** six reformers seen from the end, four filled and two open. It is the logo, the places-left marks on
  every class and the phone bar's detail.
- **Hero:** one reformer photograph (`hero.photo`, with `hero.phonePhoto` cut so the person stays in frame on a
  phone), and today's next four classes along its foot with their reformers. It settles once on load.
- **Header:** the mark beside the name, large and italic; pages right; "Book a reformer" underlined.
- **Section openings:** the name in italic, its line beside it. No labels above headings.
- **Home:** the week, the intro card (punched once per class), classes, first class and the finder, the reformer
  clip, gift cards, then the shared sections; questions move to Plan your visit and reviews to the private page.
- **Footer:** the crest, the name large in italic, address, hours, pages, the Sitefloor credit.
- **Phone bar:** the crest; the next class with its reformers, and Book (or the waitlist). The page reserves the bar's
  height, so it never covers anything.
- **Buttons:** words underlined in the shadow brown; dark ink pills only for Pay and Send.
