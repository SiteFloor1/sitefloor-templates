# Logbook (tourism)

A traveller's logbook for independent tour operators. Home: one journey, day by day (photograph · entry and stops ·
the country drawn with the route), the journey of the month with four journeys beneath as an index, then the year
drawn (journeys as lines across the months), the journeys, the ATOL number, questions and the office.

- Ground #F3F2EF, ink #141516, accent #A3561A (lichen on Patagonian granite: underlines, the chosen stop, the current
  journey, the year's selected month). Photo grade: cool-neutral, colour pulled back.
- Type: Familjen Grotesk only, 500 at up to 70px tight against 400 at 13–16px.
- Edges square; hairline rules; no shadows. Motion: the route draws over 1.6 s, photographs cross-fade over 1.2 s,
  words swap in 0.2 s; nothing on scroll; all off with reduced motion.
- Header: square stamp, inline pages, Your trip, "Plan with us →". Phone bar: Call | WhatsApp in two halves.
