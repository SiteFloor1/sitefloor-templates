A residence shown like a grand hotel's letterhead. Approved by the owner on 26 Nov 2026 (design round 1, direction A,
in `sitefloor-internal/docs/trades/residences/`).

- **Header:** centred letterhead (key-fob monogram, name, the hero kicker as one line), pages split either side,
  Reserve on the right. Phones: name left, Menu.
- **Hero:** one 2.39:1 cinema-band photograph inset in the ivory page (4:5 on phones), the stay bar docked over its
  foot on Signature; on Starter and Plus the band stands alone with the intro under it.
- **Sections:** one Imbue line with its sentence beside it, never a label above.
- **Type:** Imbue 500/600 (name, headings, prices) and Inria Sans 400/700. **Colour:** ivory #F6F3EC, ink #1D1C19,
  fig-leaf green #3F5236 (from a fig leaf). **Motif:** a double piping line like embroidered hotel linen, on the stay
  bar, the phone bar and the footer.
- **Grade:** warm lamplight, blacks held down (the client's own photographs keep theirs).
