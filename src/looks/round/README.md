# Look: The round

A children's ballet school built on the Royal Albert Hall's one shape (kit `ballet`; demo Wrenna).

- **Hero idea:** the stage inside a great circle that runs off the page, with the school's year (terms, half terms, shows,
  exams) set round it like the frieze round the Hall. Still: nothing on it moves. Beside it the class finder: tap the
  child's age, see the class, day, time and length, ask for a trial.
- **Rhythm:** ivory ground, every photograph a circle, alternating sides; sections open with one hairline sentence with
  a heavy phrase, never a label above a heading.
- **Type:** Hahmlet alone: 200 against 360 for reading and 560 to 700 for weight. Size-matched fallback in `fonts.css`.
- **Colour:** ivory #F4F0E8 and ink #1B1613; one accent from a horse chestnut fresh out of its shell, #7B3A1E (a client's
  own colour replaces it through `brand.accent`).
- **Header:** emblem (two rings, the Hall from above) and name; "Trial class" and "Menu" (a sheet with every page).
  **Footer:** the rings again with the name and address set round them, the hours, the pages, the Sitefloor credit.
  **Phone bar:** the timetable in plain type, "Trial class" in ink.
- **Overrides:** visit, Plan your visit, questions, reviews and general pages are drawn here, not borrowed.
- **Photographs:** children only from behind, or small and far away on a stage, unless the school has consent.

References (what came from each): the Royal Albert Hall itself (the round; the frieze round the outside), its website
(dates first), Wigmore Hall (quiet, type-led pages), Shakespeare's Globe (the wooden O: the circle as the only shape).
Design round: `sitefloor-internal`, `docs/trades/ballet/design-r1/`.
