# Look: Atelier (cakes kit)

A cake designer's studio in daylight. Demo: **Pastillage** (`sites/demos/cakes`). The directions, references and the
comparison with every other look are in `sitefloor-internal/docs/trades/cakes/directions.md`.

- **Palette from one gilded sugar magnolia leaf:** ivory `#F6F3EE` (page), cream `#FFFDF9` (bands, footer), ink
  `#231E19` (text, the filled button, the phone bar), gilt `#8A5F1E` (underlines, the tier rules, the chosen slice;
  AA on both grounds). Photographs carry one warm, gilt-leaning grade.
- **Type:** Chonburi (one weight) only where the type is huge: the guest count, the tiers in type (the stacked
  sizes widen like a cake's tiers), h1 and h2. Golos Text 400–600 for everything else, including the name, buttons,
  labels and h3, so the serif never spreads into the small print. Size-matched fallbacks in `fonts.css`.
- **The mark:** three tier rules, smallest on top, the base in gilt. It is the logo and the favicon, and the same
  rule sits under each tier size in the servings answer.
- **Hero:** the hero cake (`hero.photo`, `hero.phonePhoto`, `hero.piece`) fills the left of the screen top to
  bottom, its number and price along its foot. Beside it the servings calculator (Plus and up): the number huge,
  and the answer built in type, a line per tier sized to its width. On Starter, the studio's line and a message link.
- **Header:** the mark and the name in heavy type; pages, then WhatsApp. On the home page (wide screens) it sits over
  the right-hand column only, beside the cake.
- **Section openings:** the heading alone, heavy and very large; a line under it. No labels.
- **Home:** Choose by the slice, three commissions (compact rows on a phone), Is your date free?, then the shared
  sections; the studio's visit details live on Plan your visit. About six phone screens (an e2e guard). Servings, the tasting
  box, the venue check, the care card, the process, the lead time and consultations move to their pages when on.
- **Footer:** the name heavy, then label-and-value rows like a cake's label (studio, hours, ask, pages); the credit.
- **Phone bar:** ink; the live answer from the calculator ("3 tiers · 120 guests") and Send on WhatsApp.
- **Buttons:** words underlined in gilt; one ink button per section at most.
