# Palm (skin kit)

A grand-hotel spa, in daylight. The pages split either side of the clinic's name in widely spaced capitals, a
palm-green rule beneath; one large framed photograph; then "How does your skin feel today?" answered by a printed
treatment card in the clinic's own words. Ivory ground, palm-green accent, one fine gold line; Antic Didone and
Baskervville, roman only. Footer as a letterhead; the phone bar like the foot of a menu card.

- `site.hero`: `kicker` (one line under the photograph), `photo` (`src`, `width`, `height`, `alt`, `focus?`),
  `phonePhoto?` (an upright crop for phones), `credit?` (shown on the photograph). Without the finder (Starter),
  `title` and `intro` sit under the photograph as the page's heading.
- Accent: any of the suggested colours, or the clinic's own if it passes contrast on ivory.
