# Look: Marese (lingerie kit)

A Mayfair lingerie house at night, on a pale ground. Approved by the owner on 29 Oct 2026 after four rounds of
mockups; the brief, references, casting rules and plan split are in `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/lingerie-directions.md`.

- **Palette, one morello cherry:** blossom `#F3ECE8` (page), tissue `#FBF7F4` (panels), stone `#1A0A0C` (ink),
  **oxblood `#4A0F17`** (the fruit: the one signature colour, always in full blocks: the strip, the main action,
  the box, the phone bar), blossom in shade `#D9C2BD` (words on oxblood or photographs).
- **Type:** Bellefair (display, very large; the logotype in spaced capitals), Karla (everything else).
- **Header:** an oxblood strip with the shop's line (a CSS-only boutique/online switch on demos with
  `shop.previewModes`), services left, the logotype large and centred, the salon or the shop and *Ask* right, the
  categories beneath. Phones: logotype centred, *Menu* right, categories swipe.
- **Hero:** two campaign photographs edge to edge (`hero.photos`), the collection small in oxblood, the line large
  beneath, two actions. Nothing is laid over the lingerie.
- **Section openings:** the name in Bellefair on the left, its line small on the right.
- **Chapters:** full-width close crops with a few words very large over a shaded edge.
- **Photography:** one campaign and one grade (lamplit, warm, deep shadow); the page stays light. Casting: editorial
  models, polished, no visible tattoos or piercings, the lingerie as the subject, no nudity.
- **Motion:** a slow 1.03 zoom on doors and piece photographs on hover; nothing tied to the scroll.
- **Phone bar:** oxblood, *Book a fitting* left, *Ask* right.
