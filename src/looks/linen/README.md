# Linen

For the trainer kit (independent personal trainers and private studios). A private studio, by appointment.

- **Hero:** one plain line (`hero.title`) and one sentence (`hero.intro`), then the diary: the trainer's week as a
  printed appointment page from their sheet (`diary`, see the trainer-diary README). Spoken-for hours stay blank, gone
  hours shade, open hours carry ink and a walnut rule. A photograph of the trainer (`diary.photo`) sits over a block of
  spoken-for hours; the count of open hours joins the sentence once the sheet is read.
- **Type:** Ibarra Real Nova 400, 600 and 400 italic throughout. It has lining figures but no tabular ones, so every time
  sits in equal-width digit boxes (`.tn`) and reads down the page like an appointment book.
- **Colour:** linen ground, warm black ink, one walnut accent (open-hour rules, numerals, the chosen option). Client
  accents from `brand.accent.suggested`.
- **Header:** key-tag M and name left; pages and "Reserve an hour" right. **Phone bar:** the next private hour and Reserve.
- **Footer:** an ink band with the address, the week's hours and the pages.
- **Motion:** open hours fade in once; nothing follows the scroll.
