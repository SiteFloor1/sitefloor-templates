# Capsule (pop-up kit)

A brand's pop-up as a printed invitation, for brand launches, tasting rooms, sample sales and touring installations.

- **Hero:** the product, lit, in a tall dark slab (`hero.photo`, with `small` for phones); beside it a double-ruled card
  with the monogram (the business's first letter in a bottle-neck outline with a quince capsule bar), the brand's line,
  the dates, the place, the days left and the run's live line (`popup-run`, and `popup-line` when on).
- **Home:** the rooms and how long a visit takes, timed entry (a day strip and a grid of times), the next opening and
  the waitlist, Instagram, questions, the address.
- **Type:** Bona Nova (display) and Inclusive Sans (reading), claimed in `docs/trades/pop-up/fonts.md`.
- **Colour:** chalk ground #F3F1EC, ink #17150F, accent quince #C08A1E (text in #7E5A0E). Clients choose their own
  accent (`brand.accent`).
- **Footer:** an alcohol brand's "Please drink responsibly." with the Drinkaware link (`alcohol`), the pages, email and
  phone, the Sitefloor credit. Phone bar: today's line and Book a time.
