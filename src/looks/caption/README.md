# Look: The atelier wall

A day nursery hung like an exhibition (kit `nursery`; demo Alnwood).

- **Hero idea:** the children's work full width, one piece at a time, with a gallery caption card (number, title, room,
  area of learning) overlapping its foot; arrows turn the pieces, nothing turns by itself. The name and pages sit under
  the work like a museum label, then the line, large, in the roman.
- **Rhythm:** gallery-stone ground; sections open with one sentence in the roman, never a label above a heading; rooms
  read as a list of works; fees, the week's cost, the address and the Ofsted report sit on caption cards with a rosehip rule.
- **Type:** Gentium Book Plus alone: roman for reading and for what is said large, italic for titles and captions, 700
  for a few words. Size-matched fallback in `fonts.css`.
- **Colour:** stone #ECE9E3, card #F7F5F1, ink #23211E; one accent from a rosehip, #A3402A (a client's own colour
  replaces it through `brand.accent`).
- **Header:** on the home page the label strip sits under the work; on inner pages it opens the page: pebble and name,
  four pages, "Come and look round" (a WhatsApp request), Menu on a phone. **Footer:** the last wall label: name,
  address, hours, every page, the Sitefloor credit. **Phone bar:** the next place for the age picked, and "Come and look round".
- **Overrides:** visit, Plan your visit, questions and general pages are drawn here, not borrowed.
- **Photographs:** rooms, materials, the garden and the children's work; children never identifiable (from behind, far
  away, or hands at work); no names or initials on the work.

References (what came from each): the Loris Malaguzzi International Centre, Reggio Emilia (the atelier and the work
documented on the walls: the caption card), Kettle's Yard, Cambridge (stones, light, the calm stone ground), Tate
Modern's wall labels (number, title, details under a hairline), Les Petites Étoiles (materials over toys), N Family Club
(few, confident words). Design round: `sitefloor-internal`, `docs/trades/nursery/design-r1/`.
