# Length (hair kit)

A flagship hair salon where the hair is the only colour on the page. The first screen is the price by length: the
salon's own photograph of long hair, cut at the length you tap; only the hair below the line falls away (a hand-cut
layer of the same photograph) and the price and time for that length and stylist level change beside it. Then every
texture side by side, the stylists by their work and the colour year.

- Ground ivory #F8F6F1, ink #1A1A18; accent rosemary #4D5A46 (the hair-oil herb), only on the cut line, the chosen
  length, links and the colour year. Photos: ivory split-tone grade, 14%.
- Type: Darker Grotesque 300 (headings, the price; never below 28px) and 600 (the wordmark); Public Sans 400/500.
- Header: name, open now, three pages, Menu, ink Book. Footer: a tape-measure rule, the pages as a ruled index, the
  name very large. Phone bar: Prices | Book.
- Square edges. Motion only when you cut (off with reduced motion).
- Design notes and the difference table: `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/hair.md`. Demo: `sites/demos/hair` (Selwyn Orr).
