# Flannel (tailor kit)

A bespoke tailor's cutting room in daylight. Ivory and ink; the photographs carry the colour. The wood pigeon's
breast is the accent, used only as a stitch (the cloth book's spine, the current stage), a hairline (Book) and focus.

- **Hero:** the cut on the board edge to edge (`hero.cut`, 1900×1393; `hero.cutPhone`, 4:5), the coat on its stand
  (`hero.stand`, 3:4) laid over its lower corner with a slight turn, settling once on load; the one line beneath.
- **Type:** Della Respira for headings and the house's name, never below 28px (its hairlines thin out smaller);
  Commissioner 300–500 for everything else, including cloth names, labels and captions.
- **Home order:** cloth book, commission, garments, the process, then visit. Reviews and gallery move to the team page
  and the process to its own page when those are on (`onPages`), so the home page stays to about six phone screens.
- **Phone bar:** ivory with an ink hairline, "By appointment" and the ink Book button.
- **Footer:** ink, the house's name large.

Design notes, references and the difference table: `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/tailor.md`.
