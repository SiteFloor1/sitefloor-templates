# Look: Taroub (modest kit)

A gallery hang for one garment. On a wide screen the home page is three columns: the header as a left wall (the
shop's name, a second piece small with its label, the pages written as two sentences, "Questions? WhatsApp us"),
the abaya full height in the middle, and its label on the right (name, one line, price, Shop and Find your length).
Below it, once and on the same columns, the hem line. A quiet ivory bar carries the shop's first look and its total
on every screen. Other pages: the same parts in one line along the top. Phone: the name and "Menu", the photograph,
the label; the bar keeps the look and WhatsApp in reach.

- Ground warm ivory `#F6F1E9`, ink `#211C18`; accent saffron `#9A561B` (a crocus stamen), for underlines, the chosen
  length and the gap to the floor only. Photographs graded warm to the hero's tan floor.
- Type: Shippori Mincho (names, prices, answers), Afacad (everything else); size-matched fallbacks in `fonts.css`.
- Hero settings (`site.hero`): `piece` (the piece shown), `photo`, `credit`, and `side` (`piece` and `photo`) for the
  wall's second piece. `title` is the piece's name, `intro` its one line.
- References: Selhaya (one robe, few words), Bouguessa (minimal couture, the garment as the page), Effa (warm
  editorial ground), a gallery's wall label, a tailor's hem gauge.
