# Portland (law kit)

A private-client law firm in a grand London house, in Portland stone and low sun. Design, references and the approval
history: `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/law-firm.md`.

- **Hero:** one sentence (`hero.title`) in Marcellus capitals beside a tall photograph of the firm's own room
  (`hero.photo`, 4:5; a panelled library or meeting room). "Speak to a partner" under it, then the SRA digital badge
  (`regulation.badgeFrame`) with the regulated-by line.
- **Header:** the seal (the firm's initials, blind-embossed) and the name in spaced capitals; the pages, the telephone
  number and "Speak to a partner". **Phone bar:** two halves on the full-sun stone; the page keeps room for it.
- **Home order:** practice index (beside `practice.photo`), discreet arrival (beside `arrival.photo`), speak to a
  partner, regulation plate, visit. People, the photo band and matters move to their pages when those are on;
  questions and page links move to Plan your visit.
- **Palette:** one block of Portland stone: lit face `#E3DED3`, full sun `#EDE9E0`, soot `#25262A`, mortar
  `#C6C0B3`, carved shadow `#5C6470` (the one colour; a client's own accent replaces it).
- **Type:** Marcellus 400 / Source Sans 3 300–500, self-hosted, with size-matched fallbacks.
- **Photographs:** one stone grade on every photo (saturation 0.4, blacks to soot, highlights to stone). Portraits
  only neutral and credible, never fashion stock.
- **Motion:** the call to action's line lengthens, an index row shifts 14px on hover. Nothing tied to the scroll.
