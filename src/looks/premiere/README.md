# Look: Première (weddingfilm kit)

A wedding film studio's site as a premiere. Demo: **Larchmere** (`sites/demos/weddingfilm`). The design contract,
references and the comparison with every other look are in `sitefloor-internal/docs/design/wedding-film.md`.

- **Ground and accent:** ivory `#F8F3E9` (page), a deeper ivory `#F1EADC` (bands), ink `#1B140C` (text, the phone
  bar), amber `#8A5713` from fossil resin (links, the chapter line, the golden-hour band, prices; never a fill), pale
  amber `#D9B47A` on ink. The film carries the colour; the demo's footage has one warm amber grade.
- **Type:** Sorts Mill Goudy, roman and italic (the name, statements, the light sentence, chapters in italic,
  prices in italic) and Urbanist 400/600. Size-matched fallbacks in `fonts.css`.
- **Hero:** the studio's film edge to edge at 2.39:1 (4:5 on phones), muted, the still first, Pause beside the
  chapters; on Signature the chapters as a programme (`showreel`). One statement under it.
- **Header:** the name in Goudy italic; pages right; "Check your date" underlined in amber; WhatsApp quietly last.
- **Section openings:** the name in Goudy, its line beneath; no kicker, no rule. The light planner has no visible
  heading: the sentence is the section.
- **Home:** the film, the date check, the light, reviews, visit; `onPages` moves packages, the builder and delivery
  to Packages, Instagram to Photographs and the questions to Plan your visit.
- **Footer:** ivory, one hairline, the name very large in Goudy italic, address, hours, pages, the Sitefloor credit.
- **Phone bar:** ink; "Check your date" left, WhatsApp right in pale amber. The page pads its foot so it never covers content.
- **Buttons:** words with a line under them; no filled buttons, no rounded corners.
